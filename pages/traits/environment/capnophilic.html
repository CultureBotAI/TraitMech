capnophilic

METPO:1005021 · CLASS · SEEDED

A phenotype describing an organism that requires elevated concentrations of carbon dioxide for growth.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.1111/1462-2920.13092
    Campylobacter jejuni, the leading cause of human bacterial gastroenteritis, requires low environmental oxygen and high carbon dioxide for optimum growth

    Al-Haideri et al. support elevated carbon dioxide as a growth condition required by capnophilic Campylobacter jejuni.

  • DOI:10.1111/1462-2920.13092
    Taken together, our data suggest CanB is a major contributor to the capnophilic growth phenotype of C. jejuni.

    Al-Haideri et al. describe the elevated-CO2 requirement as a capnophilic growth phenotype and identify CanB as a major contributor in C. jejuni NCTC 11168.
