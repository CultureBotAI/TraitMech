arsenic tolerant

traitmech:000017 · CLASS · REVIEWED

A metalloid tolerance in which an organism grows in the presence of elevated arsenic (arsenite/arsenate) concentrations, typically via the ars operon, whose ArsB pump extrudes arsenite from the cytoplasm.

Arsenic tolerance via ars-operon arsenite efflux

Evidence-backed causal sketch linking the ars operon and ArsB pump to extrusion of cytoplasmic arsenite.
