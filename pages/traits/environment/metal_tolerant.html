metal tolerant

traitmech:000012 · CLASS · REVIEWED

An environmental tolerance in which an organism grows in the presence of elevated concentrations of toxic heavy-metal or metalloid ions, typically via efflux-based resistance determinants (RND-family CBA pumps, P-type ATPases, and cation diffusion facilitators).

Metal tolerance via active efflux of heavy-metal ions

Evidence-backed causal sketch linking heavy-metal exposure to efflux-pump-mediated detoxification underlying broad metal tolerance.
