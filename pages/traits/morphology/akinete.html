akinete

traitmech:000185 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A morphology trait in which a filamentous cyanobacterium differentiates enlarged, thick-coated, spore-like dormant cells called akinetes that can germinate into vegetative cells.

Trait evidence (3)

  • DOI:10.1159/000517443
    Some cyanobacteria of the order Nostocales can form akinetes, spore-like dormant cells resistant to various unfavorable environmental fluctuations.

    Garg and Maldener support akinetes as spore-like dormant cells formed by Nostocales cyanobacteria under unfavorable environmental conditions.

  • DOI:10.1159/000517443
    Akinetes are enveloped in a thick protective coat containing a multilayered structure and are able to germinate into new vegetative cells under suitable growth conditions.

    The source supports the thick protective coat and vegetative-cell germination clauses in the local definition.

  • DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2025.1677844
    Raphidiopsis raciborskii (Nostocales) is a successful invader of temperate ecosystems originating from the tropics that forms akinetes in their new habitats to overcome unfavorable winter conditions.

    Rios-Henriquez and Weithoff provide a second Nostocales cyanobacterium study supporting akinete formation as an unfavorable-condition dormancy trait.
