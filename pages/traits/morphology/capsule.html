capsule

traitmech:000063 · CLASS · REVIEWED

A morphology trait in which the cell is surrounded by a well-organized layer of polysaccharide (or rarely polypeptide) external to the cell envelope, mediating adhesion, desiccation resistance, and immune evasion.

Capsule polysaccharide layer mediates adhesion and immune evasion

Evidence-backed causal sketch linking capsular polysaccharide biosynthesis to a surface polysaccharide layer that mediates adhesion, desiccation resistance, and immune evasion.
