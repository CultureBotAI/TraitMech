expansion-driven sliding motility

traitmech:000703 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phenotype in which a microbial population spreads across a supporting surface through passive displacement of cells by expansive forces generated within the colony, without active locomotory propulsion of the displaced cells.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.1111/1758-2229.70214
    Passive sliding motility allows Mycobacterium smegmatis to spread over soft agar surfaces.

    Pollitt et al. 2025, PMID:41152213, PMC12568390; scientific abstract, not graphical abstract. Full text, Figure 1 and the actual DOCX supplement were inspected. Results 2.8-2.9 interpret digitate-colony expansion as hydraulic displacement, not a direct force measurement; unobserved division does not establish absent growth. Supplementary Methods identify mc2 155. The Figure 1 comparison varies both gel material and concentration, not material alone. The qualified definition includes this interpretation without making cell-division pressure universal. Raw movies were not reanalyzed. Qualified MED, Crossref and the retrieved publisher page exposed no linked correction or retraction at the 2026-10-11 check.

  • DOI:10.1186/1471-2180-6-31
    sliding motility is a passive mode of translocation for spreading over surfaces.

    Fall et al. 2006, PMID:16545127, PMC1501027; Background's source-attributed terminology, not an abstract quote. Results and Methods directly describe surface expansion of constructed B. subtilis DS64 hag-replacement material. Growth-associated, surfactin-dependent spreading supports the conventional case, not a universal transporter or surfactant requirement. Actual Figure 1 says 24 hours, whereas its caption and Results say 18 hours; retain this unresolved discrepancy. Proposed plant-root benefit is not observed natural colonization. Qualified MED, Crossref and the retrieved publisher page exposed no linked correction or retraction at the 2026-10-11 check.

Retain the qualified expansion-driven scope without settling broader sliding terminology.

CONTROVERSY OPEN expansion-sliding-scope-and-naming · raised by codex · 2026-10-11

Not yet attached to a section of this record — a curator sets attaches_to (e.g. causal_graphs#some_edge) so the gap shows beside the mechanism it concerns.

The label is deliberately qualified: Kovacs et al. include gravity-assisted droplet surfing under sliding, whereas Hennes et al. distinguish it by driving force. Neither unqualified sliding nor surfing is asserted as an exact synonym here. Translation of an entire droplet under gravity alone is outside this expansion-driven definition. Fall's Background uses motility as a broader umbrella than current METPO:1000701 independent movement and METPO:1000702 energy-dependent independent movement; phenotype is therefore the conservative parent. This is not swimming, active gliding, swarming or twitching, and does not require absence of locomotory appendages from the organism. Different modes can coexist; no organism-level disjointness is asserted. Colony diameter, wetting, bulk advection or a sequence feature alone is insufficient. The letters document terminology, not additional experimental replication.
