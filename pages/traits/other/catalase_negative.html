catalase negative

METPO:1007084 · CLASS · SEEDED

Test-outcome phenotype where the catalase test yields a negative result (no bubbling on H2O2). The underlying enzyme-organism relation should additionally be asserted via <organism> METPO:2000303 'does not show activity of' GO:0004096 'catalase activity'.
