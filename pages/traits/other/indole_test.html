indole test

METPO:1005010 · CLASS · SEEDED

An assay that tests the ability of an organism to produce indole from tryptophan.

Trait evidence (2)

  • https://asm.org/protocols/indole-test-protocol
    The indole test screens for the ability of an organism to degrade the amino acid tryptophan and produce indole.

    The ASM protocol defines the indole test as an assay for organism-level tryptophan degradation to indole.

  • https://asm.org/protocols/indole-test-protocol
    The chief requirement for culturing an organism prior to performing the indole test is that the medium contains a sufficient quantity of tryptophan (5).

    The protocol explains that the indole-test medium needs tryptophan so production of indole can report tryptophan degradation.
