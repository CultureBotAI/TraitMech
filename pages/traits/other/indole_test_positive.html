indole test positive

METPO:1005011 · CLASS · SEEDED

A phenotype in which an organism tests positive in the indole test, indicating it produces indole from tryptophan.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.1128/AEM.02787-15
    Indole, a bacterial product of tryptophan degradation

    Darkoh et al. support microbial indole production as a tryptophan-degradation phenotype and evaluate indole detection in bacterial cultures.

  • DOI:10.1128/AEM.02787-15
    enterotoxigenic Escherichia coli strain H10407 produces 3.3 ± 0.22 mM indole during a 24-h period in the presence of 5 mM tryptophan

    Darkoh et al. measured indole production by the enterotoxigenic E. coli H10407 strain under tryptophan-supplemented culture conditions.
