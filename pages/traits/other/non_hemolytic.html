non-hemolytic

METPO:1005027 · CLASS · SEEDED

A phenotype in which an organism does not lyse red blood cells.

Trait evidence (1)

  • DOI:10.14202/vetworld.2025.3622-3630
    Hemolysis testing revealed γ-hemolysis for all strains, confirming non-hemolytic and non-pathogenic properties.

    Petjul et al. examined hemolytic patterns on Columbia agar supplemented with 5% sheep blood and reported gamma hemolysis for all assayed Bacillus isolates, supporting non-hemolysis as absence of red-blood-cell lysis on blood agar.
