fried-egg-shaped colony

METPO:1007069 · CLASS · SEEDED

A colony shape that has a raised opaque centre and a translucent peripheral zone, resembling a fried egg.

Trait evidence (1)

  • DOI:10.1371/journal.pone.0068373
    TR colonies showed the typical “fried egg” appearance, with a small opaque center surrounded by a large translucent peripheral area.

    Bertin et al. describe the opaque-center, translucent-periphery colony appearance that underlies the MICRO-derived fried-egg colony definition.
