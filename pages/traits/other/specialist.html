specialist

traitmech:000177 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phenotype describing an organism with a narrow ecological niche, restricted to a limited range of environments or resources.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.1093/femsec/fiaa240
    Organisms are often categorized as generalists or specialists, corresponding to broad or narrow niche requirements

    Bell and Bell frame specialists as organisms with narrow niche requirements, contrasting them with bacterial generalists.

  • DOI:10.1038/s41559-023-02027-7
    Generalists can survive in many environments, whereas specialists are restricted to a single environment.

    The social-niche-breadth study defines specialists as environmentally restricted prokaryotes while contrasting them with generalists.
