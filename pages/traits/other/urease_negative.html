urease negative

METPO:1007088 · CLASS · SEEDED

Test-outcome phenotype where the urease test yields a negative result. The underlying enzyme-organism relation should additionally be asserted via <organism> METPO:2000303 'does not show activity of' GO:0009039 'urease activity'.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.5812/chbs-160199
    A positive reaction is indicated by the entire liquid medium and the surface of the solid medium turning pink, whereas a negative reaction is characterized by the medium remaining yellow

    Hafezi and Khamar review the urease-test polarity and interpret an unchanged yellow medium as a negative assay result.

  • DOI:10.5812/chbs-160199
    Both types contain a phenol red reagent that changes color with pH variations

    The review describes the pH-indicator readout shared by liquid and solid urease-test media.
