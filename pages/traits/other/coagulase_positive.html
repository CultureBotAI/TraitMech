coagulase positive

METPO:1007090 · CLASS · SEEDED

Test-outcome phenotype where the coagulase test yields a positive result (plasma clotting). The underlying enzyme-organism relation should additionally be asserted via <organism> METPO:2000302 'shows activity of' <coagulase enzyme term> once a sufficiently specific enzyme term is selected (no GO/EC term currently exists at the bacteriological coagulase test granularity).

Trait evidence (3)

  • DOI:10.1099/mic.0.000019
    coagulase-positive S. aureus bacteria promoted clotting of plasma which was not seen when a coagulase-deficient mutant strain was used

    Loof et al. show that coagulase-positive S. aureus clots plasma in a way lost from a coagulase-deficient mutant, directly supporting the positive test-outcome phenotype.

  • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1001036
    Clinical isolates of the human pathogen Staphylococcus aureus secrete coagulase (Coa), a polypeptide that binds to and activates prothrombin

    Cheng et al. connect S. aureus Coa secretion to prothrombin activation, the clotting activity detected by the coagulase test.

  • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1001036
    Coa and vWbp promote the non-proteolytic activation of prothrombin and cleavage of fibrinogen, reactions that are inhibited with specific antibody against each of these molecules.

    Cheng et al. demonstrate that Staphylococcus aureus staphylocoagulase (Coa) and von Willebrand factor-binding protein (vWbp) promote non-proteolytic activation of prothrombin and cleavage of fibrinogen.
