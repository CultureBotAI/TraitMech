colony shape

METPO:1007063 · CLASS · SEEDED

A colony morphology characterized by the overall macroscopic colony outline as observed on solid medium.

Trait evidence (2)

  • https://asm.org/asm/media/protocol-images/colony-morphology-protocol.pdf
    Describe the form, elevation, and margin as indicated in Fig. 1. Also indicate whether the colonies are smooth (shiny glistening surface), rough (dull, bumpy, granular, or matte surface), or mucoid (slimy or gummy appearance).

    Breakwell et al. describe colony form as one of the primary macroscopic morphology descriptors recorded for well-separated colonies on solid medium.

  • https://asm.org/asm/media/protocol-images/colony-morphology-protocol.pdf
    FIG. 1. Diagram illustrating the various forms, elevations, and margins of bacterial colonies (3).

    The ASM protocol separates colony forms from elevations and margins, supporting this record as the colony-outline component of colony morphology.
