catalase test

METPO:1007080 · CLASS · SEEDED

A biochemical test that detects catalase enzyme activity by exposing cells to hydrogen peroxide and observing for visible bubbling. The test outcome (positive or negative) is captured by its child classes; this class itself does not assert that the organism has catalase activity.

Trait evidence (2)

  • https://asm.org/protocols/catalase-test-protocol
    The catalase test facilitates the detection of this enzyme in bacteria

    The American Society for Microbiology catalase-test protocol describes the bacterial assay context for detecting catalase.

  • DOI:10.1038/srep03081
    The enzyme-generated oxygen bubbles trapped by Triton X-100 were visualized as foam

    Iwase et al. support the visible oxygen-bubble readout produced by catalase-mediated hydrogen-peroxide decomposition.
