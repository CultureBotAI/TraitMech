coagulase negative

METPO:1007091 · CLASS · SEEDED

Test-outcome phenotype where the coagulase test yields a negative result. The underlying enzyme-organism relation should additionally be asserted via <organism> METPO:2000303 'does not show activity of' <coagulase enzyme term> once a sufficiently specific enzyme term is selected.
