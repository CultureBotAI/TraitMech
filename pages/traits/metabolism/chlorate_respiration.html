chlorate respiration

traitmech:000198 · CLASS · PROPOSED

An anaerobic respiration in which an organism uses chlorate as the terminal electron acceptor and reduces it to chloride for energy conservation.

Trait evidence (3)

  • DOI:10.1128/AEM.69.9.5585-5592.2003
    chlorate ion as the sole electron acceptor for oxidation of organic matter in a previously unknown mode of respiration

    Danielsson Thorell et al. support the organism-level respiratory use of chlorate as the electron acceptor.

  • DOI:10.1128/AEM.69.9.5585-5592.2003
    reduction of chlorate occurs in a two-step reaction: chlorate reduction followed by chlorite decomposition

    The Ideonella dechloratans chlorate pathway reduces chlorate and then decomposes the chlorite intermediate.

  • DOI:10.1128/AEM.07303-11
    Perchlorate-respiring bacteria (PRB) can use both perchlorate and chlorate as terminal electron acceptors, whereas chlorate-respiring bacteria (CRB) use only chlorate

    Hellberg Lindqvist et al. separate chlorate-respiring bacteria from broader perchlorate reducers.
