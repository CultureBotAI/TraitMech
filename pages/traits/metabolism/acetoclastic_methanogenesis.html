acetoclastic methanogenesis

traitmech:000191 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A methanogenesis in which acetate is split into methane and carbon dioxide.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.1007/s00253-023-12700-3
    the acetoclastic methanogens split acetate to CH4 and CO2

    Szuhaj et al. distinguish acetoclastic methanogens by their acetate-to-methane-and-carbon-dioxide branch of methanogenesis.

  • DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2017.01198
    acetoclastic methanogenesis using acetate

    Evans et al. describe acetoclastic methanogenesis as the acetate-using branch among major methanogenic carbon-source pathways.
