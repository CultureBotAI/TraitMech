anaerobic ammonium oxidation

traitmech:000188 · CLASS · PROPOSED

An anaerobic nitrogen metabolism in which ammonium is oxidized with nitrite as the electron acceptor to form dinitrogen.

Trait evidence (3)

  • DOI:10.1038/22749
    This new process combines ammonia and nitrite directly into dinitrogen gas

    Strous et al. identified the anaerobic ammonia oxidation process and linked ammonia plus nitrite conversion to dinitrogen formation.

  • DOI:10.1128/AEM.65.7.3248-3250.1999
    The affinity constants for the substrates ammonium and nitrite were each less than 0.1 mg of nitrogen per liter.

    Strous et al. measured ammonium and nitrite as high-affinity anammox substrates.

  • DOI:10.1038/nature10453
    anammox converts ammonia and nitrite into dinitrogen (N2) gas

    Kartal et al. review the pathway scope and support anammox as the established exact synonym.
