pectin degradation

traitmech:000135 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A biopolymer-degradation metabolism in which an organism depolymerizes pectin into oligogalacturonides and catabolizes the released pectin breakdown products using pectinolytic enzymes.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.1128/MMBR.00038-07
    Catabolism of pectin and its breakdown products by pectinolytic bacteria occurs within distinct cellular environments

    Abbott and Boraston review pectin breakdown by pectinolytic Enterobacteriaceae as a multicomponent microbial catabolic pathway.

  • DOI:10.1146/annurev.micro.50.1.213
    The extracellular degradation of the pectin leads to the formation of oligogalacturonides that are catabolized through an intracellular pathway

    Hugouvieux-Cotte-Pattat et al. review the pectinase system and pectin-catabolite pathway of Erwinia chrysanthemi strain 3937.
