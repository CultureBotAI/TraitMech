pectin degradation

traitmech:000135 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A biopolymer-degradation metabolism in which an organism depolymerizes pectin into oligogalacturonides and catabolizes the released pectin breakdown products using pectinolytic enzymes.

Compartmentalized pectin depolymerization and oligogalacturonide catabolism
