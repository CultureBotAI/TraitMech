selenate respiration

traitmech:000202 · CLASS · PROPOSED

An anaerobic respiration in which an organism uses selenate as the terminal electron acceptor for energy conservation.

Trait evidence (4)

  • DOI:10.1128/aem.60.8.3011-3019.1994
    A gram-negative, strictly anaerobic, motile vibrio was isolated from a selenate-respiring enrichment culture

    Oremland et al. isolated strain SES-3 from a selenate-respiring enrichment culture and showed anaerobic growth coupled to selenate reduction.

  • DOI:10.1128/AEM.02737-06
    use selenate as a terminal electron acceptor

    Narasingarao and Haggblom define the phenotype as use of selenate as a terminal electron acceptor in dissimilatory selenate reduction.

  • DOI:10.1128/AEM.02737-06
    four novel anaerobic dissimilatory selenate-respiring bacteria

    Narasingarao and Haggblom support selenate respiration as a repeatedly observed microbial trait across aquatic-sediment isolates rather than a single strain-specific anomaly.

  • DOI:10.1016/j.syapm.2005.12.011
    Here we characterize a novel selenate-respiring bacterium, strain AK4OH1, isolated from an estuarine sediment enrichment culture

    Narasingarao and Haggblom described Sedimenticola selenatireducens AK4OH1 as an anaerobic selenate-respiring bacterium isolated from estuarine sediment.
