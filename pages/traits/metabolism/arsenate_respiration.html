arsenate respiration

traitmech:000195 · CLASS · PROPOSED

An anaerobic respiration in which an organism uses arsenate as the terminal electron acceptor and reduces it to arsenite for energy conservation.

Trait evidence (3)

  • DOI:10.1073/pnas.1834303100
    can be used by microorganisms as a terminal electron acceptor in anaerobic respiration

    Saltikov and Newman define the organism-level scope as microbial anaerobic respiration using arsenate, As(V), as a terminal electron acceptor.

  • DOI:10.1128/AEM.69.5.2800-2809.2003
    This gram-negative strain stoichiometrically couples the oxidation of lactate to acetate with the reduction of As(V) to arsenite

    Saltikov et al. isolated and characterized ANA-3 as a facultatively anaerobic As(V)-respiring Shewanella strain.

  • DOI:10.1128/JB.01110-07
    the Shewanella sp. strain ANA-3 arsenate respiratory reductase (ARR), the key enzyme involved in this metabolism

    Malasarn et al. biochemically characterize ARR as the arsenate respiratory reductase for ANA-3 arsenate respiration.
