complete ammonia oxidation

traitmech:000187 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A nitrification metabolism in which a single organism oxidizes ammonia via nitrite to nitrate.

Trait evidence (3)

  • DOI:10.1038/nature16459
    Complete oxidation of ammonia to nitrate in one organism (complete ammonia oxidation; comammox) is energetically feasible

    van Kessel et al. define complete ammonia oxidation, or comammox, as oxidation of ammonia to nitrate in a single organism.

  • DOI:10.1038/nature16461
    Here we report on the discovery and cultivation of a completely nitrifying bacterium from the genus Nitrospira, a globally distributed group of nitrite oxidizers.

    Daims et al. independently demonstrated complete nitrification in a single Nitrospira organism.

  • DOI:10.1128/aem.01698-23
    The dominance of Nitrospira sp. BO4 could be explained by the ability of comammox to generate more energy through the complete oxidation of ammonia to nitrate

    Ghimire-Kafle et al. support the same ammonia-to-nitrate scope in a freshwater complete-ammonia-oxidizing Nitrospira enrichment.
