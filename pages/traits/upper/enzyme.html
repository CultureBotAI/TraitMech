enzyme

METPO:1000527 · CLASS · REVIEWED

A biological macromolecule (typically a protein, occasionally a catalytic RNA) that catalyses a specific biochemical reaction. This class is the material-entity sense of "enzyme" (the molecule itself), matching METPO:1000527's placement as a subclass of METPO:1000186 (material entity). Used as the range class for METPO enzyme-related OBJECT_PROPERTYs (shows_activity_of, enzyme_activity_analyzed, does_not_show_activity_of).
