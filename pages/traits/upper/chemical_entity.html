chemical entity

METPO:1000526 · CLASS · REVIEWED

A material entity that is a physical entity of interest in chemistry, including molecular entities, parts thereof, and chemical substances. Used as the range class for the majority of METPO chemical-use OBJECT_PROPERTYs (uses_as_carbon_source, uses_as_electron_donor, ferments, oxidizes, etc.) so that organism-to-chemical assertions resolve to a curated chemical class.
