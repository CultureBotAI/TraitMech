fungal nematophagy

A nutritional phenotype in which a fungus consumes nematodes as food.

Native DOI abstract verifier: UNRESOLVED, retained separately from the direct match. Crossref and USDA identify the same DOI/title.

    Figure 2a caption, actual PDF page 6 and panel inspected, with Methods 2.1-2.3 and Results 3.2. This is a source-named feeding observation, not measured nutrient flux. Native verifier: UNRESOLVED. Host susceptibility is differential; neither all nematodes nor all Pleurotus isolates are asserted. The two excerpts from this paper total 17 words.

  • https://horizon.documentation.ird.fr/exl-doc/pleins_textes/fan/40780.pdf

  • DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.3002400
    At mid to late time points, we observed hyphal growth inside nematodes following penetration of the cuticle, corresponding to digestion.

Protease inhibition delays internal growth at 12 h without proving complete feeding loss or quantifying assimilation. Remaining figures and raw S1 spreadsheet were not fully audited.

Feeding on living or previously dead nematodes is included; fungal killing, obligacy and starvation are not universal requirements. Mere contact, paralysis, killing, trap formation or a sequence annotation alone is insufficient. Fungal nematode-trap formation (traitmech:000718) is structural, neither necessary nor sufficient for feeding. Mycoparasitism (traitmech:000717) has fungal prey; predatory bacterium (traitmech:000054) has a bacterial actor and prey. Phagotrophy (traitmech:000628), myzocytosis (traitmech:000631) and pallium feeding (traitmech:000632) impose different uptake modes. Saprotrophy (traitmech:000055) can overlap, without organism-level disjointness. Local parasitism (traitmech:000043) inherits persistent association, not required here. Keep phenotype METPO:1000059 pending a closer feeding hierarchy; no universal carbon, nitrogen or energy contribution is specified. Bare nematophagy includes nonfungal actors and is not an exact synonym. No unverified synonyms or xrefs are asserted.
