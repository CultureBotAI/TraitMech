fungal nematode endoparasitism

traitmech:000727 · CLASS · PROPOSED

An animal-pathogenic phenotype in which a fungus uses spores attached to or ingested by a vermiform nematode to initiate infection and then grows inside the host.

Trait evidence (3)

  • DOI:10.1371/journal.pgen.1006017
    Drechmeria coniospora is an obligate fungal pathogen that infects nematodes via the adhesion of specialized spores to the host cuticle.

    Lebrigand et al. 2016, PMID:27153332, PMCID:PMC4859500, scientific Abstract. Direct JATS match and native VERIFIED. Author Summary and Discussion distinguish spore-initiated endoparasitism from hyphal traps and egg infection. Actual Figures 8 and 9 show recombinant fungal growth in C. elegans, not unmodified natural exemplars. S1 Text was fully read and visually checked: the laboratory line was serially passaged for about 15 years; its source isolate and ARSEF 6962 share provenance. Obligate is the authors' species description, not a universal class requirement or inability to grow on their artificial medium. Predicted genes and expression are not a universal mechanism.

  • DOI:10.3390/jof10010068
    Then, the fungal hyphae penetrate into the nematode body cavity and grow in the nematode body

    Sun et al. 2024, PMID:38248977, PMCID:PMC10820206, Results 3.2. Direct JATS match; native NOT_IN_ABSTRACT retained. Methods 2.5 and actual Figure 2c,d concern GFP-engineered HR-eGFP1 on Bursaphelenchus xylophilus; Figure 2 distinguishes attachment, entry, internal growth and emergence. The entire two-page supplement, including S1-S3, was inspected. Methods 2.6 scores attached spores or colonization as parasitism; no rate is imported as an internal-infection rate. Main-text intestinal infection after swallowing is tentative; do not silently strengthen it using the supplement caption. No field efficacy or safety is inferred.

  • DOI:10.1017/S0953756296002092
    The infection method for Harposporium cycloides was shown to be by spore ingestion.

    Glockling and Shimazu 1997, publisher scientific Abstract. Direct contiguous HTML match; DOI/title confirmed by Crossref and Europe PMC AGR:IND20619532. The native abstract resolver returned UNRESOLVED because it returned no abstract; this is not a missing-index claim. The primary abstract reports spore-ingestion infection in Harposporium cycloides and infectivity testing after pure culture. Full Methods, figures and host/strain provenance were not accessed; no canonical taxon, dose, route universality or pure-culture obligacy is inferred.

Refine the infection-strategy hierarchy without conflating infection, trapping and feeding.

CURATION TODO OPEN fungal-nematode-endoparasitism-scope-and-hierarchy · raised by codex · 2026-10-14

Not yet attached to a section of this record — a curator sets attaches_to (e.g. causal_graphs#some_edge) so the gap shows beside the mechanism it concerns.

The fungal bearer has the established spore-infection-strategy phenotype, not merely an anatomical location inside a nematode. The host-contacting or ingested spore is the infectious unit; spore germination into an external hyphal trap is not this route. Adhesion or ingestion alone, killing alone, internal occupancy without the specified route and sequence annotation are insufficient. Vermiform stages distinguish the selected strategy from eggshell penetration in fungal nematode egg parasitism traitmech:000726. Fungal nematophagy traitmech:000719 requires feeding and includes dead prey; fungal nematode trap formation traitmech:000718 concerns hyphal differentiation. Neither is asserted as a parent. Local parasitism traitmech:000043 inherits persistent symbiosis, not required here. Animal pathogen METPO:1004002 is active below pathogenic to host METPO:1004000 and phenotype METPO:1000059. Current upstream clarifies its pinned organism-like wording as a phenotype; the local parent remains unchanged. No organism-level disjointness, exclusive host stage, universal host range or obligacy is asserted. Bare endoparasitism does not specify the fungal actor, host or infection route and is not an exact synonym.
