epibiotic bacterial predation

traitmech:000697 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A predatory bacterial phenotype in which a bacterium kills and obtains nutrients from bacterial prey while attached and growing externally, without its cell body entering the prey cell.

Trait evidence (3)

  • DOI:10.1038/ismej.2013.164
    epibiotic, where they attach to the prey from outside.

    Pasternak et al. 2014, PMID:24088628, PMC3930308, Introduction; contiguous quotation defining the external predation strategy. Abstract, Introduction, culture/prey-range/electron-microscopy Methods, growth Results, Discussion and actual Figure 1 read. Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3930308/.

  • DOI:10.1099/ijs.0.039701-0

    Koval et al. 2013, PMID:22368169, directly read scientific Abstract on the publisher site and source-qualified MED metadata. JSS was isolated from sewage in London, Ontario, with Caulobacter crescentus prey; attached external predation left emptied cells. This supports natural strain provenance and noninvasive feeding, not its frequency in undisturbed sewage. Full text, figures and supplement unavailable to this review; no Methods claim imported. Publisher: https://doi.org/10.1099/ijs.0.039701-0.

The 2025 correction DOI:10.1038/s41467-025-58758-7 (PMID:40234419, PMC12000597) was read: retain its qualified prey-range wording and removal of the S-layer-defense recommendation. Predatory bacterium traitmech:000054 supplies killing and nutrition; external attachment and growth supply the differentia. The broader METPO:1007093 epibiont phenotype does not require predation. Epibiosis, ectosymbiosis, ectoparasitism and vampirism are not asserted exact synonyms. The existing predation research report also calls wolf-pack Myxococcus feeding epibiotic; this record follows the attached-feeding sense of Pasternak et al., not every use of external killing. Attachment, prey lysis or sequence signatures alone are insufficient. Bacterial cannibalism traitmech:000626 concerns prey relatedness, an orthogonal axis. No organism-level disjointness is asserted. No exact xrefs or synonyms are assigned pending authority and scope reconciliation.

Resolve feeding machinery without generalizing model-specific observations.

KNOWLEDGE GAP OPEN epibiotic-predation-mechanism-limits · raised by codex · 2026-10-11

Not yet attached to a section of this record — a curator sets attaches_to (e.g. causal_graphs#some_edge) so the gap shows beside the mechanism it concerns.

Causal graphs and protein examples are deferred, not claimed absent. An external predator cell body does not exclude envelope-crossing feeding structures or secreted effectors. JSS imaging supports a feeding junction but does not identify its molecular machinery; fluorescence loss is not direct nutrient tracing. Comparative gene content is not functional causality. Do not require polar attachment, binary division, fixed progeny number, a universal prey range, or obligate dependence merely because particular models exhibit them. The corrected 2024 S-layer experiments concern tested prey backgrounds, not all predators or all S-layers. The 2013/2014 studies share authors and JSS material; three citations are not three independent laboratory replications.
