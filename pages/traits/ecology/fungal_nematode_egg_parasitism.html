fungal nematode egg parasitism

traitmech:000726 · CLASS · PROPOSED

An animal-pathogenic phenotype in which a fungus infects nematode eggs by penetrating their eggshells and colonizing their interiors.

Trait evidence (3)

  • DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2021.617710
    The egg-pathogenic fungus Purpureocillium lilacinum parasitizes on nematode eggs

    Xu et al. 2021, PMID:33897634, PMCID:PMC8058359, scientific Abstract; direct JATS match and native VERIFIED. Own JZD02 experiment, Methods and actual Figure 1 A-C support infection of Meloidogyne incognita eggs. Supplementary Table 1 defines infection by hyphal penetration through an individual egg, not mere surface contact. Its 30-hour printed mean disagrees with the replicate counts; no numerical infection rates are adopted. The 6-hour control and 42-hour infected transcriptomes differ in time and include mixed infection stages. Expression associations are not causal protein evidence.

  • DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2016.01084
    Infected eggs by P. lilacinum 36-1.

    Xie et al. 2016, PMID:27486440, PMCID:PMC4949223, Figure 5 caption, panel B; actual panels A-C and Methods/Results inspected. P. lilacinum denotes Purpureocillium lilacinum. Direct JATS match; native NOT_IN_ABSTRACT is retained. This is its own 36-1 infection experiment, not a repetition of Xu's citation. Eggs were supplied as the only nourishment for the reported 24-hour comparison, but microscopy does not quantify nutrient assimilation. RNA-seq replicates were pooled; gene homology and expression do not demonstrate a required effector.

  • DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2021.617710
    Infection defined by Purpureocillium lilacinum mycelium penetrating through the whole individual Meloidogyne incognita egg

    Xu et al. 2021, Supplementary Table 1 footnote, Table_1.DOCX from the PMC8058359 Europe PMC supplementaryFiles archive. Direct DOCX text match and complete rendered page inspected; native NOT_IN_ABSTRACT is retained. This operational scoring criterion supports individual-egg penetration, not another independent experiment or a direct nutrient-flux measurement. The two Xu excerpts total 23 words. The printed 30-hour mean discrepancy remains unresolved; no infection rate is imported.

The fungal bearer penetrates eggshells and colonizes egg interiors. Surface attachment, egg-hatching inhibition, killing, trapping or a gene annotation alone is insufficient. Animal pathogen METPO:1004002 is active below pathogenic to host METPO:1004000 and phenotype METPO:1000059. Its pinned organism-like wording is clarified as a phenotype in current upstream METPO; the local parent is not rewritten. Nematode egg infection does not entail mammalian pathogenicity. Xie describes this fungus as nematophagous, but the operational infection endpoint does not quantify feeding: fungal nematophagy traitmech:000719 requires consumption and also includes dead prey. It is related, not an asserted parent here. Local parasitism traitmech:000043 inherits persistent symbiosis, which is not required. No organism-level disjointness, obligacy, exclusive host stage or universal host range is asserted. Bare egg parasitism does not specify the fungal actor and is not an exact synonym.
