epiphytic

traitmech:000443 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A host-associated trait in which a microbe resides on living aerial plant surfaces.

Plant-surface colonization defines epiphytism

Evidence-backed ecological sketch connecting exposed living plant surfaces to epiphytic residence.

NONMECHANISTIC · This broad plant-host habitat trait spans bacteria, yeasts, and filamentous fungi on external plant surfaces and does not assert one universal molecular colonization mechanism.

Edge evidence

  • living external plant surface enables epiphytic colonization RO:0002327

    Living aerial plant surfaces are the host tissue habitat where epiphytic populations establish.

    • DOI:10.1128/AEM.69.4.1875-1883.2003 The aerial habitat colonized by these microbes is termed the phyllosphere, and the inhabitants are called epiphytes. Lindow and Brandl define the phyllosphere as an aerial plant habitat and its inhabitants as epiphytes.
  • epiphytic colonization confers epiphytic METPO:2007700

    Epiphytic colonization realizes the plant-surface epiphytic ecological trait.

    • DOI:10.1186/s12915-024-01967-1 Epiphytic microbes are those that live for some or all of their life cycle on the surface of plant leaves. Fones et al. define epiphytic microbes by leaf-surface residence.
