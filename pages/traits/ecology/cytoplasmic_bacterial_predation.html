cytoplasmic bacterial predation

traitmech:000700 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A predatory bacterial phenotype in which a bacterium enters the cytoplasm of a bacterial prey cell and grows and reproduces there by consuming and degrading the prey cytoplasm.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.1073/pnas.83.7.2138
    Daptobacter penetrates both membranes of the prey cell walls and reproduces in their degrading cytoplasm.

    Guerrero et al. 1986, PMID:11542073, PMC323246, Figure 2E caption continued on p. 2141. Predatory bacterium traitmech:000054 supplies the broader trophic phenotype. Periplasmic bacterial predation traitmech:000699 has a different replication compartment; epibiotic bacterial predation traitmech:000697 keeps the predator cell body external. Generic endobiotic or intracellular predation, endoparasitism and endosymbiosis are not exact synonyms. Guerrero et al. discuss predator/parasite and primary-consumer terminology on pp. 2141-2142; the operational feeding/location definition here does not settle that broader ecological vocabulary. Entry alone or intracellular growth without destructive prey consumption is insufficient. No organism-level disjointness, fixed prey range or obligate prey dependence is asserted.
