alpha-fucosidase activity

traitmech:000152 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological enzyme-activity phenotype in which a cell produces active alpha-fucosidase enzymes that hydrolyze alpha-L-fucosides to L-fucose and an alcohol.

Trait evidence (2)

  • https://iubmb.qmul.ac.uk/enzyme/EC3/2/1/51.html
    Reaction: an α-L-fucoside + H2O = L-fucose + an alcohol

    The NC-IUBMB EC 3.2.1.51 entry defines alpha-L-fucosidase by its accepted name and hydrolytic reaction, grounding the enzyme activity named by the organism-level phenotype.

  • DOI:10.1128/jcm.22.3.333-335.1985
    Positive reactions for alpha-glucosidase, beta-glucosidase, alpha-fucosidase, and alpha-glucuronidase suggest that "B. forsythus" may be saccharolytic.

    Tanner et al. detected alpha-fucosidase in the then-proposed oral species Bacteroides forsythus with the API ZYM enzyme activity panel.
