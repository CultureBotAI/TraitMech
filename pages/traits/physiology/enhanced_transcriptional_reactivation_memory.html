enhanced transcriptional reactivation memory

traitmech:000713 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological phenotype in which a microbial cell or lineage retains a non-genetic state from prior inducing conditions that enhances the rate or magnitude of later transcriptional activation after intervening repression.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.7554/eLife.77646
    Upon Anchor Away of Opi1, reactivation was faster than activation

    Sump et al. 2022, PMID:35579426, PMC9129879; Results s2-1, paragraph 2, contiguous in primary XML and publisher Version of Record HTML. Figure 1D uses synthetic Opi1 Anchor Away to bypass upstream induction control; enhancement is lost in mrs and nup100 mutants. Ordinary-starvation INO1 reactivation in Figure 1B is not clearly faster than activation. Figure 1 supplement 1C separately measures CHO1/ACT1 after three-hour repression; its generic statistical-comparator caption is ambiguous. Figure 1 supplement 2E measures 18-hour peripheral localization, not that duration of enhanced transcription; the main Results mispoints to supplement 1. Figure 1G competitive fitness is a different endpoint, not proof of transcriptional causality. Selected Results, Methods, scientific abstract, actual figures and strain-table rows were inspected. RT-qPCR mRNA kinetics are not direct measurements of initiation rate. Native abstract lookup returns NOT_IN_ABSTRACT; direct full-text matching supports the quotation, not a native VERIFIED verdict.

  • DOI:10.1534/genetics.112.143016
    Cells with prior NaCl exposure have a faster gene-expression response to H2O2.

    Guan et al. 2012, PMID:22851651, PMC3454879; complete opening sentence of the Figure 4 caption, contiguous in publisher HTML with chemical subscripts retained as text. BY4741-background laboratory yeast received 0.7 M NaCl for 60 minutes, washing and 240 minutes of stress-free YPD recovery (two generations in Results), then 0.5 mM H2O2, compared with mock-treated cells. No significant pre-challenge expression difference at FDR 0.05 does not prove identical baselines. Figure 4 includes induced and repressed genes; only the enhanced-induction subset supports this activation phenotype. The 449-gene count is not a count of induced genes. Methods and Figure 4A sample through 40 minutes, whereas Results describes a 60-minute response. Figure 5 shows TSA2 mRNA kinetics by qPCR; nup42 deletion reduces the priming benefit, while nup100 naive and primed responses are both fast, not simple evidence of a universal mechanism. Four-to-five generation resistance persistence is a different endpoint. Relevant Results, Methods, scientific abstract and actual Figures 4 and 5 were inspected; raw data were not reanalyzed. Native abstract-verifier results are retained separately in the source sidecar; exact publisher full-text matching supports this caption quotation.

Carbon-source memory (traitmech:000706) spans growth and induction endpoints and is not reparented wholesale. Memory of acquired stress resistance (traitmech:000712) concerns retained resistance, not faster transcription. Anticipation and bet hedging are also not exact equivalents. Unqualified transcriptional memory, epigenetic memory and priming are not asserted as exact synonyms.
