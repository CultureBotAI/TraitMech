trypsin activity

traitmech:000158 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological enzyme-activity phenotype in which a cell exhibits trypsin-like serine endopeptidase activity, preferentially cleaving peptide bonds on the carboxyl side of arginine or lysine residues.
