diatom perizonium production

traitmech:000693 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A biomineralization phenotype in which a diatom produces the siliceous band system called the perizonium in an auxospore wall.

Trait evidence (3)

  • DOI:10.1371/journal.pone.0272778
    The deposition of the primary TP band became evident when the auxospore center attained a slim and rigid appearance.

    Kaczmarska et al. 2022, PMID:36067191, PMC9447881, Results: Epizonium and perizonium. TP means transverse perizonium. Scientific Abstract, Introduction, Methods, early Results, this subsection and Discussion: Auxospore structure and development read in official Europe PMC XML on 2026-10-10; actual Figure 11 inspected. Scaly-band architecture and proposed shape control are not universal or demonstrated necessities.
