lethal nematicidal activity

traitmech:000720 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological capability of a microorganism to kill nematodes.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.1038/s41598-023-33669-z
    T. asperellum FbMi6 at 80% concentration had the highest (90.3%) juvenile mortality compared to control.

    Saharan et al. 2023, PMID:37088805, Results in vitro activity; native NOT_IN_ABSTRACT, direct publisher HTML match. Actual Figure 4 inspected. This is culture filtrate, not live fungal inoculum: 100 newly hatched M. incognita J2, water/PDB controls, 72 h endpoint, 25 +/- 2 degrees C, two repeats. Methods reports fresh-water recovery confirmation without its duration or scoring criterion. The quote's percentage is source-reported, not independently recalculated. Supplement text/tables and its embedded image were inspected, not rendered document pages. Strain/accession discrepancies prevent a canonical taxon assignment; pot/field efficacy is not imported.

  • DOI:10.1128/spectrum.02577-21
    In this study, three secondary metabolites (SMs) with nematicidal activities were isolated from in vitro cultures of P. luminescens sonorensis strain Caborca

    Producer-origin supporting evidence, not definition authority. The self-contained excerpt establishes microbial origin of nematicidal metabolites; its wording alone does not distinguish death from paralysis. The independently cited Saharan mortality result supplies explicit lethal definition support. Kusakabe et al. 2022, PMID:35138171, Discussion opening clause, printed/PDF page 12; native NOT_IN_ABSTRACT and direct XML/typeset PDF match. The producer is Photorhabdus. Culture-supernatant fractionation links the isolated metabolites to this microorganism. t-CA and PPA caused mortality; indole mainly caused reversible paralysis at lower doses, with killing only at high concentrations. Equipotent synthetic standards were used for dose-response assays. Actual Figures 2/3 and Table 4 inspected. Needle response and morphology distinguished death from paralysis; recovery used washing and 24 h in water. Table 4 tests lower-dose indole, not t-CA/PPA survival. No live-culture lethal concentration is inferred. Some numerical prose conflicts with tables; no potency, selectivity or significance values are adopted. Linked supplemental NMR/statistics files were not audited; figure inspection is not raw-data verification.
