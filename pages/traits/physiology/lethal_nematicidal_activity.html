    PPA and t-CA displayed potent, concentration-dependent nematicidal activities against the root-knot nematode (Meloidogyne incognita) and the citrus nematode (Tylenchulus semipenetrans)

Linked supplemental NMR/statistics files were not audited; figure inspection is not raw-data verification.
