alpha-galactosidase activity

traitmech:000149 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological enzyme-activity phenotype in which a cell produces active alpha-galactosidase enzymes that hydrolyze alpha-1,6-linked galactose residues in oligosaccharides and polymeric galactomannans.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.3109/07388551.2013.794124
    The enzyme α-galactosidase (α-D-galactoside galactohydrolase; EC 3.2.1.22) catalyzes the hydrolysis of α-1,6-linked galactose residues in oligosaccharides and polymeric galactomannan.

    Katrolia et al. define alpha-galactosidase as EC 3.2.1.22, an enzyme that hydrolyzes alpha-1,6-linked galactose residues, grounding the enzyme activity named by the organism-level phenotype.

  • DOI:10.1128/jcm.22.3.333-335.1985
    Selenomonas sputigena was positive for α-galactosidase, β-galactosidase, and α-glucosidase (API ZYM strip) and for α-glucosidase, β-glucosidase, and α-galactosidase (API An-Ident strip).

    Tanner et al. detected alpha-galactosidase in Selenomonas sputigena with the API ZYM enzyme activity panel and the API An-Ident strip.
