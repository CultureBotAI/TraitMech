nematode-repelling activity

traitmech:000725 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological capability of a microorganism to elicit active movement of nematodes away from itself or substances it produces.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.1073/pnas.0610281104
    C. elegans specifically avoids certain strains of Serratia based on their production of the cyclic lipodepsipentapeptide serrawettin W2.

    Pradel et al. 2007, PMID:17267603, PMCID:PMC1892944, scientific Abstract; exact normalized PMC match and native VERIFIED. C. elegans denotes Caenorhabditis elegans. Own Results and actual Figures 1-4 distinguish initial entry followed by leaving Serratia marcescens lawns from absence of attraction. A swrA insertion mutant shows reduced lawn leaving but similar multiday killing; reduced avoidance is not loss of pathogenicity. Purified serrawettin W2 on E. coli OP50 lawns and acute drop-induced reversal are separate assays. The 16-hour bacterial-lawn endpoint, 60-minute supplemented-lawn endpoint and acute reversal are not one exposure. Chemical recognition versus a mechanical surfactant cue remains unresolved, and other bacterial cues can contribute. Serratia swrA is not the similarly named Bacillus motility regulator.

  • DOI:10.15252/embj.2020106938
    a specific volatile organic compound, 1‐undecene, produced by the pathogen P. aeruginosa is sensed as an aversive signal by C. elegans.

    Prakash et al. 2021, PMID:34086368, PMCID:PMC8246062, Discussion first paragraph, own-study result; directly matched JATS, PMC and publisher text. Native NOT_IN_ABSTRACT is retained, not relabeled VERIFIED. This is its own experiment, not a citation of Pradel. P. aeruginosa denotes Pseudomonas aeruginosa. Figure 1A-B measures PA14 lawn occupancy over 12 hours. Pure 1-undecene gives negative chemotaxis and omega turns in distinct Figure 2 and Appendix S2 assays. PA14-versus-undA odor choice establishes relative preference, not by itself repulsion or complete loss of bacterial repellence. Peer-review reply page 14 reports reversals but no complete lawn leaving after 1-undecene on OP50; the alternative adjacent-lawn crossing assay is not eviction from an occupied lawn. Roaming or higher mean square displacement alone is not directional avoidance. Recipient sensory genetics is not a microbial causal pathway.
