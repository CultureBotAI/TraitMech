acid phosphatase activity

traitmech:000142 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological enzyme-activity phenotype in which a cell produces active acid phosphatase enzymes that dephosphorylate phosphate-containing compounds under acidic conditions.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.1128/JB.182.23.6850-6853.2000
    the presence of acid phosphatase activity in the culture filtrate of M. tuberculosis was confirmed and a 28-kDa protein possessing this activity was purified and characterized

    Saleh and Belisle confirmed Mycobacterium tuberculosis acid phosphatase activity and purified the SapM protein carrying that activity.

  • DOI:10.1155/2021/8888641
    According to the API ZYM assays, all three isolates were positive for alkaline phosphatase, leucine aryl amidase, acid phosphatase, and naphthol_AS_BI_phosphohydrolase.

    Mekonnen et al. treated acid phosphatase as an assayed bacterial exoenzyme phenotype in rapid urease-producing soil isolates.
