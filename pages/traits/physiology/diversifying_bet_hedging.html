diversifying bet hedging

traitmech:000708 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological phenotype in which a clonal microbial lineage reversibly interconverts between alternative cell states independently of the prevailing environment and before environmental change, spreading risk across unpredictable environmental changes through state-specific survival or reproductive trade-offs.

Trait evidence (3)

  • DOI:10.1038/nature08504
    Here we report the de novo evolution of bet hedging in experimental bacterial populations.

    Beaumont et al. 2009, PMID:19890329; scientific abstract, directly checked against publisher and source-qualified MED text, not the publisher's Editorial Summary. Supports experimentally evolved risk spreading by rapid phenotype switching under a changing selection regime. Mutational evolution of a switching genotype is not the same claim as mutations generating each alternative cell state. Main figures, supplements and raw data were not independently assessed; no gene-level mechanism or natural canonical exemplar is asserted.

  • DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.1001325
    Our results suggest that yeast bet hedging results from multiple epigenetic growth states determined by a combination of stochastic and deterministic factors.

    Levy, Ziv and Siegal 2012, PMID:22589700, PMC3348152; scientific abstract, directly checked against publisher, MED and full-text XML, separately from the Author Summary. Box 1 supplies the definition framework and explicitly leaves adaptive origin untested in this yeast study. The record preserves that limit: reversible state variation and differential stress survival do not alone establish evolutionary optimization. Binary states and a purely stochastic mechanism are not required.

  • DOI:10.1038/s41467-024-46297-6
    Mobile cells grow faster but are stress-sensitive, while non-mobile cells prioritise stress resistance over growth.

    Tang et al. 2024, PMID:38453919; scientific abstract, directly checked against publisher and source-qualified MED text. The Introduction and Discussion distinguish early diversification of Haematococcus pluvialis cultures from later stress-induced conversion. Growth and survival are fitness proxies, not a demonstration of optimized long-term geometric mean fitness. Figures, supplements and raw data were not independently assessed; proposed transcriptional explanations are not encoded as causal edges.

The diversifying qualifier restricts this class to alternative states within a clonal microbial lineage; it does not cover every use of unqualified bet hedging. Heterogeneity, noisy gene expression or switching alone is insufficient: reversible states must precede the relevant environmental shift and differ in survival or reproductive performance across conditions. Generic stress-induced change, carbon-source memory, cue-triggered environmental anticipation, genetic resistance and ecological generalist/specialist niche breadth are not exact synonyms. Particular observations can overlap without making these classes equivalent or disjoint. Dormancy and persister formation can participate in risk spreading, but their presence alone does not establish it, so neither existing record is reparented. METPO:1000059 phenotype is the conservative parent pending upstream review of this lineage-level physiological disposition. This is not a sequence feature, individual assay result or mixed-species community property. Following Levy et al. Box 1, the relevant state interconversion must be independent of the prevailing environment; temporal precedence alone does not establish this criterion. Cue-triggered preparation before a later change is not sufficient. Deterministic age-related variation can qualify, so environmental independence does not require a purely stochastic switching mechanism.

Assess adaptive origin, natural exemplars and molecular mechanisms separately from the operational phenotype.
