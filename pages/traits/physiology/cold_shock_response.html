cold shock response

traitmech:000206 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A stress response in which a rapid temperature downshift induces nucleic-acid-binding cold-shock proteins and RNA-remodeling functions that preserve gene expression at low temperature.

Cold-shock RNA adaptation

Evidence-backed causal sketch linking abrupt temperature downshift to bacterial cold-shock protein induction and RNA chaperone support for low-temperature translation.

MECHANISTIC · This graph captures the conserved CspA-family RNA-support branch of the bacterial cold-shock response without making one cold-sensing regulator, one RNA helicase, or one post-transcriptional circuit universal across bacteria.
