bacterial polar flagellar ejection

traitmech:000732 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological phenotype in which a bacterium actively ejects previously assembled axial structures of a polar flagellum by separation at its base.

Trait evidence (3)

  • DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.3000165
    Plesiomonas cells consistently lose their flagella at the base of the hook

    Ferreira et al. 2019, PMID:30889173, PMC6424402. Results, Shed hook-filament structures accumulate in P. shigelloides supernatant, sole paragraph (HTML anchor article1.body1.sec2.sec4.p1; PDF pp. 4-5); Figure 1G. Direct publisher full-text match; native NOT_IN_ABSTRACT. Hook-bearing released structures, population counts and relic/assembly comparisons support ejection rather than daughter-cell dilution or passive midfilament breakage. Nutrient-depletion conditions vary among assays; minimal medium containing glucose is not complete carbon starvation. All 25 main PDF pages and six figures were inspected, not raw supplementary data. Energy conservation and leakage prevention are interpretations.

  • DOI:10.1111/mmi.14511
    bacteria can actively abandon flagella after prolonged stationary culturing

    Zhuang et al. 2020, PMID:32259388, scientific abstract, sentence beginning Interestingly. Direct MED/publisher match and native VERIFIED. Main publisher text and six figure captions were subsequently read, not the actual figures or supplement. Sections 2.4-2.7 separate daughter-cell dilution from stationary-phase shedding. OD times flagellated fraction is a population proxy, not a direct cell count. VIO5 and engineered EGFP-FliG strain NMB344 are distinct; red/green panels in Figure 3 are not the same cells. Two-color experiments in Figures 5-6 support basal release, but rod breakage is inferred, not a directly imaged cut. Surface immobilization and medium exchanges constrain the assay.

This qualified phenotype requires active basal release of previously assembled polar flagellar axial structures. Hook-filament release is an observed endpoint, not a universal component-completeness requirement. Flagellar possession or arrangement, assembly failure, motor arrest, reduced motility, a decreasing flagellated fraction or passive filament breakage alone do not establish it. Periplasmic resorption and microbial-eukaryotic axonemal autotomy are distinct. Phenotype METPO:1000059 is a definition-based parent inference; locomotory possession is not a required parent. The definition does not require every flagellum to be lost, nonmotility, a particular stimulus, exact severing site, relic morphology or universal behavior among polar-flagellated bacteria. No bare shedding or deflagellation exact synonym is asserted.
