bacterial flagellar filament internalization

traitmech:000735 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological phenotype in which a bacterium withdraws an assembled portion of its own flagellar filament from outside its cell body into its cell-body periplasm.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.1038/s41564-023-01401-2
    most or all of the filament was internalized to the periplasm

Direct manuscript JATS match; native NOT_IN_ABSTRACT retained. Source strain HD100 and frozen cryo-ET stages support partial through nearly complete withdrawal into the predator's own periplasm, not the prey's. Methods S8-S12 qualify minicell enrichment and possible physiology effects; the images contain no same-cell temporal information. Publisher Figure 3's main scale bar is 100 nm, versus 50 nm in the manuscript caption; no dimension or rate is inferred. Main PDF, other actual main figures, unselected supplemental images and movies remain unread. Later breakage or degradation is not required by the definition.

Review the withdrawal endpoint without making universal entry-time claims.

CURATION TODO OPEN bacterial-flagellar-internalization-scope · raised by codex · 2026-10-16

Not yet attached to a section of this record — a curator sets attaches_to (e.g. causal_graphs#some_edge) so the gap shows beside the mechanism it concerns.

Cell-body periplasm distinguishes withdrawal from an external flagellar sheath lumen, static endoflagella, abnormal assembly, import of free flagellin, uptake of another cell's material and predator entry into the prey's periplasm. Partial withdrawal counts; permanent intactness and complete withdrawal are not required. Bacterial polar ejection traitmech:000732 releases structures externally; eukaryotic axoneme internalization traitmech:000734 involves a different apparatus. These distinctions do not imply disjoint organisms. Earlier 109J observations of externally retained filaments constrain universal early-entry claims but neither demonstrate own-periplasmic withdrawal nor establish its absence. Different strains and preparations are not pooled into one time series.
