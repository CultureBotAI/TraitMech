alpha-chymotrypsin activity

traitmech:000159 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological enzyme-activity phenotype in which a cell exhibits chymotrypsin-like serine endopeptidase activity, preferentially cleaving peptide bonds on the carboxyl side of tyrosine, tryptophan, phenylalanine, or leucine residues.
