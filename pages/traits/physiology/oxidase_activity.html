oxidase activity

traitmech:000076 · CLASS · REVIEWED

A physiological enzyme-activity phenotype in which a cell produces a terminal respiratory oxidase (notably cytochrome c oxidase); it is the basis of the diagnostic oxidase test.

Terminal respiratory oxidase reducing oxygen

Evidence-backed causal sketch linking a terminal respiratory oxidase to reduction of molecular oxygen, the basis of the oxidase test.

    • DOI:10.3390/microorganisms10050926 Hederstedt: "three copper atoms (CuA is di-copper in subunit II; CuB in subunit I)."
