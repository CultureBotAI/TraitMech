NAD-dependent alcohol dehydrogenase activity

traitmech:000165 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological enzyme-activity phenotype in which a cell produces active NAD-dependent alcohol dehydrogenases that interconvert primary or secondary alcohols with aldehydes or ketones.

Trait evidence (3)

  • https://iubmb.qmul.ac.uk/enzyme/EC1/1/1/1.html
    <b>Reaction:</b> (1) a primary alcohol + NAD<small><sup>+</sup></small> = an aldehyde + NADH + H<small><sup>+</sup></small><br> (2) a secondary alcohol + NAD<small><sup>+</sup></small> = a ketone + NADH + H<small><sup>+</sup></small>

    The NC-IUBMB EC 1.1.1.1 entry names alcohol dehydrogenase as alcohol:NAD+ oxidoreductase and defines the NAD-dependent primary-alcohol-to-aldehyde and secondary-alcohol-to-ketone reactions.

  • DOI:10.1111/j.1432-1033.1986.tb09366.x
    The two alcohol dehydrogenases found in Zymomonas mobilis have each been purified using dye-ligand chromatography and affinity elution with nucleotides.

    Neale et al. purified the ZADH-1 and ZADH-2 isoenzymes from Zymomonas mobilis and characterized their ethanol-oxidizing and acetaldehyde-reducing activities.

  • DOI:10.1128/jb.169.6.2591-2597.1987
    The gene which encodes alcohol dehydrogenase II (adhB) from Zymomonas mobilis was cloned in Escherichia coli

    Conway et al. cloned the Z. mobilis adhB gene, connected it to the purified alcohol dehydrogenase II protein, and used an indicator plate that detected recombinant ADH activity.
