bacteriocin production

traitmech:000183 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological trait in which bacteria produce bacteriocins, ribosomally synthesized antimicrobial peptides or proteins that kill or inhibit other bacteria.

Trait evidence (3)

  • DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2016.00461
    Bacteriocins are ribosomally synthesized antimicrobial peptides produced by bacteria, which have the ability to kill or inhibit other bacteria.

    Rea et al. review bacteriocins as bacterially produced ribosomal antimicrobial peptides.

  • DOI:10.1146/annurev.micro.56.012302.161024
    the most abundant and diverse family of microbial defense systems: the bacteriocins.

    Riley and Wertz support bacteriocins as a widespread, diverse microbial defense-system family.

  • DOI:10.1038/srep27973
    Traditionally, nisin was produced industrially by using Lactococcus lactis in the neutral fermentation process.

    Zhang et al. support Lactococcus lactis as an industrial nisin-producing bacterium.
