beta-N-acetylhexosaminidase activity

traitmech:000157 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological enzyme-activity phenotype in which a cell produces active beta-N-acetylhexosaminidase enzymes that hydrolyze terminal, non-reducing N-acetyl-D-hexosamine residues in N-acetyl-beta-D-hexosaminides.

Trait evidence (2)

  • https://iubmb.qmul.ac.uk/enzyme/EC3/2/1/52.html
    Reaction: Hydrolysis of terminal non-reducing N-acetyl-D-hexosamine residues in N-acetyl-β-D-hexosaminides

    The NC-IUBMB EC 3.2.1.52 entry defines beta-N-acetylhexosaminidase by its accepted reaction, grounding the glycosidase activity named by the organism-level phenotype.

  • DOI:10.1021/jf020965x
    Serratia marcescens YS-1, a chitin-degrading microorganism, produced mainly N-acetylhexosaminidase

    Kurakake et al. reported production of N-acetylhexosaminidase by Serratia marcescens YS-1, supporting this as a bacterial beta-N-acetylhexosaminidase activity phenotype.
