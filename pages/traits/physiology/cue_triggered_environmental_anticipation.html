cue-triggered environmental anticipation

traitmech:000707 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological phenotype in which exposure to an environmental cue induces changes that prepare a microorganism for a distinct, temporally associated environmental condition before that condition occurs.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.1038/nature08112
    early stimuli pre-induce genes that would be needed for later ones, yet later stimuli only induce genes needed to cope with them.

    Mitchell et al. 2009, PMID:19536156; scientific abstract, checked against publisher and source-qualified MED text. Main Figures 2-3 and supplementary Figures 3 and 6 were visually inspected, with their relevant Methods and control explanations. MG1655 sugar-switch assays distinguish early induction from subsequent growth; glycerol normalization and residual-sugar controls matter. Loss of the association in laboratory-evolved strains concerns selection across generations, not cognitive learning. Ecological explanations remain author interpretations. Not all raw data were reanalyzed.

  • DOI:10.1038/nmicrobiol.2017.84
    Here, we identify a metabolic adaptation that is triggered upon entry into hypoxia-induced quiescence but facilitates subsequent cell cycle re-entry.

    Eoh et al. 2017, PMID:28530656, PMC5540153; scientific abstract, checked against publisher and source-qualified MED text. The abstract and qualitative main-text framing support metabolically encoded preparation, with hypoxia preceding re-aeration in the studied Mycobacterium tuberculosis system. This is not evidence for a universal transcriptional mechanism or an in-vivo demonstration of anticipation. Figures, supplements and raw data were not independently assessed; no molecular causal edges are asserted.

The cue-triggered qualifier is deliberate: solely clock-driven preparation or constitutive readiness is insufficient. Evidence must link the preceding cue to preparation for a different later condition; correlated expression or cross-protection alone does not establish that interpretation. Generic stress response, stochastic bet hedging, carbon-source memory and diauxic growth are not exact synonyms, although particular observations can overlap. Neither cognition nor learning within an individual's lifetime is required; evolutionary regulatory wiring is compatible. No universal benefit, fixed delay, asymmetric response or single mechanism is required. METPO:1000731 nutrient adaptation is locally framed as chronic copiotroph/oligotroph trophic adaptation, not this cue-to-future-condition phenotype. METPO:1000059 phenotype is the conservative parent pending upstream hierarchy review. This is an organismal disposition, not a sequence feature, regulatory site or precomposed chemical-use assertion.
