pyrazinamidase activity

traitmech:000163 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological enzyme-activity phenotype in which a cell produces active nicotinamidase/pyrazinamidase enzymes that hydrolyze nicotinamide and can convert pyrazinamide to pyrazinoic acid.

Trait evidence (3)

  • https://iubmb.qmul.ac.uk/enzyme/EC3/5/1/19.html
    <b>Reaction:</b> nicotinamide + H<small><sub>2</sub></small>O = nicotinate + NH<small><sub>3</sub></small>

    The NC-IUBMB EC 3.5.1.19 entry grounds the nicotinamide-deamidating side of the PncA activity with the accepted nicotinamidase reaction.

  • DOI:10.1111/j.1742-4658.2007.06241.x
    The nicotinamidase/pyrazinamidase (PncA) of Mycobacterium tuberculosis is involved in the activation of the important front-line antituberculosis drug pyrazinamide by converting it into the active form, pyrazinoic acid.

    Zhang et al. characterized the Mycobacterium tuberculosis PncA enzyme, linking nicotinamidase/pyrazinamidase activity to cellular conversion of pyrazinamide into pyrazinoic acid.

  • DOI:10.1371/journal.pone.0015785
    Pyrazinamidase (PncA) activates the first-line antituberculous drug pyrazinamide into pyrazinoic acid.

    Petrella et al. independently describe PncA as the pyrazinamidase that activates pyrazinamide, supporting the pyrazinamidase label for the organism-level activity.
