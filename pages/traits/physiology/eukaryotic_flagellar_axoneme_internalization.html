eukaryotic flagellar axoneme internalization

traitmech:000734 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological phenotype in which a microbial eukaryote internalizes the intact axoneme of its own cilium or flagellum into its cell body.

Review intact withdrawal separately from later axonemal disassembly.

CURATION TODO OPEN eukaryotic-axoneme-internalization-scope · raised by codex · 2026-10-15

Not yet attached to a section of this record — a curator sets attaches_to (e.g. causal_graphs#some_edge) so the gap shows beside the mechanism it concerns.

The defining endpoint is internalization of the cell's own intact eukaryotic axoneme. Static internal localization, uptake of another cell's material, external coiling, failed assembly, motor arrest, passive breakage or shedding alone do not establish it. Venard et al. 2020 place internalization within a broader cilium-disassembly program while distinguishing subsequent degradation. The operational resorption record traitmech:000733 requires shortening through axonemal disassembly, not intact withdrawal alone. Later degradation can occur after internalization; no organism-level disjointness or permanently intact internal axoneme is asserted. Autotomy traitmech:000731 and bacterial polar flagellar ejection traitmech:000732 have different endpoints; bacterial periplasmic flagella and type IV pili are different structures. Phenotype METPO:1000059 is a definition-based parent inference, without a locomotion requirement.
