eukaryotic flagellar resorption

traitmech:000733 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological phenotype in which a microbial eukaryote shortens previously assembled cilia or flagella through disassembly of their axonemes.

Trait evidence (2)

  • DOI:10.1083/jcb.100.2.457
    Cells under resorption-inducing conditions rapidly shortened their flagella

    L'Hernault and Rosenbaum 1985, PMID:3968171, PMCID:PMC2113451.1, printed page 458, Results, Monitoring of Flagellar Length during In Vivo Labeling, sole paragraph; Figure 2 on page 459. Contiguous typeset and whitespace-normalized OCR match; native NOT_IN_ABSTRACT retained. The XML body is a single OCR preformat block, not a section-structured JATS paragraph. Source-designated wild-type Chlamydomonas reinhardtii 21gr was mechanically deflagellated and pulse-chase labelled during regeneration before resorption was induced with 15 mM sodium pyrophosphate at pH 7.5 plus cycloheximide; HEPES was the control. The shortening concerns regenerated flagella, not failed assembly. Apparent flagellalessness after 90 minutes still permits the reported short stubs. Protein fractionation supports transfer to the cell body, not complete recycling. All six main PDF pages, three figures and Table I were inspected; raw data were not reanalyzed. Figure 1's two determinations and Table I's two gels per sample are not established biological-replicate counts.

  • DOI:10.1186/1471-2121-4-11
    Acid shock causes resorption of mutants unable to deflagellate.

    Parker and Quarmby 2003, PMID:12930563, PMCID:PMC194436, Figure 7 caption, first sentence, page 9 of 12; JATS F7 under sec9/sec5. Direct caption and typeset match; native NOT_IN_ABSTRACT retained. The acid-shock experiments concern Chlamydomonas fa1, fa2 and adf1 mutants, with coiling and incomplete resorption described; wild-type cells instead shed and regrow their flagella in this comparison. Figure 7 scores the fraction of cells with flagella longer than 7 micrometers, not a resorption rate or count of released organelles. The separate fla10 temperature experiments must not be merged with this assay. Low-calcium HEPES is not calcium-free: Methods retain trace calcium and do not add chelators. The main length measurements count at least 70 cells per time point and use at least three independent cultures. All twelve main PDF pages and eight figures were inspected. Proposed basal disassembly and shared severing machinery are not universal requirements of this class.

This physiological phenotype concerns shortening of a previously assembled eukaryotic axoneme. Static short flagella, failed assembly, nonmotility, bending or coiling, passive breakage, or shedding a largely intact organelle alone do not establish it. Neither complete structural disappearance nor complete protein recycling is required. Basal severing and residual transition zones can accompany resorption; the class does not require an intact base or the absence of severing. Eukaryotic flagellar autotomy traitmech:000731 and bacterial polar flagellar ejection traitmech:000732 have different endpoints. Bacterial periplasmic resorption involves a different apparatus. No organism-level disjointness is asserted. Phenotype METPO:1000059 is the definition-based parent; locomotion is not required. Palmelloid formation traitmech:000654 does not require flagellar loss.
