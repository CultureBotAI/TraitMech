lethal algicidal activity

traitmech:000695 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological capability of a microorganism to kill cells of eukaryotic algae or cyanobacteria.

The lethal qualifier distinguishes this capability from broader algicidal usage. Paul and Pohnert 2011, DOI:10.1371/journal.pone.0021032, Methods Monitoring algicidal activity and Results, uses fluorescence-based growth inhibition as an algicidal readout; those directly read passages provide terminology context, not additional killing evidence. This record requires killing, not reduced fluorescence, reversible growth arrest, ecdysis, passive removal or a protease sequence alone. It denotes the acting microbe's capability, not an algicide molecule, treated alga's susceptibility or assay row. Cyanobacteria are included explicitly, not classified as eukaryotic algae. Activity against some susceptible targets suffices; universal host range is not asserted. Predatory bacterium traitmech:000054 requires killing and consuming bacteria for nutrients, so it is not a parent of this class. Killing may overlap predation but neither consumption, grazing nor nutritional benefit is required or inferred; no organismal disjointness is asserted. No exact synonym or phenotype xref is assigned pending authority and scope review.
