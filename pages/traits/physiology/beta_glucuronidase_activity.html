beta-glucuronidase activity

traitmech:000151 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A physiological enzyme-activity phenotype in which a cell produces active beta-glucuronidase enzymes that hydrolyze beta-D-glucuronosides to D-glucuronate and an alcohol.

Trait evidence (2)

  • https://iubmb.qmul.ac.uk/enzyme/EC3/2/1/31.html
    Reaction: a β-D-glucuronoside + H2O = D-glucuronate + an alcohol

    The NC-IUBMB EC 3.2.1.31 entry defines beta-glucuronidase by its accepted name and hydrolytic reaction, grounding the enzyme activity named by the organism-level phenotype.

  • DOI:10.1128/aem.50.6.1383-1387.1985
    The beta-glucuronidase produced by E. coli cleaves the MUG substrate to yield a fluorescent end product.

    Moberg used beta-glucuronidase cleavage of MUG to detect Escherichia coli in food samples.
