GAPS2 system

traitmech:000380 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism possesses a GMT-encoded GAPS2 locus represented by DefenseFinder as a single-profile model, GAPS2__GAPS2, and experimentally linked to P1-vir and lambda-vir protection when expressed in E. coli.

Mahata et al. support GAPS2 as a GMT-encoded anti-phage defense system that protects E. coli against P1-vir and lambda-vir when expressed from a Vibrio parahaemolyticus locus, and DefenseFinder represents GAPS2 as a single-profile system.
