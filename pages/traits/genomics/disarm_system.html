DISARM system

traitmech:000211 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A genomics trait describing possession of a Defense Island System Associated with Restriction-Modification locus that uses methyltransferase-associated self/non-self discrimination and DrmAB activation to inhibit bacteriophage DNA replication.

DISARM senses phage DNA to activate DrmAB defense

Evidence-backed causal sketch linking DISARM methylation and 5-prime-overhang DNA sensing to DrmAB activation and blocked early phage DNA circularization.

MECHANISTIC · The graph captures host DNA methylation, 5-prime-overhang sensing, DrmAB activation, and early phage DNA restriction without asserting the unresolved downstream nuclease or replication-block coupling.

Edge evidence

  • DrmAB core complex contributes to DISARM DrmAB activation RO:0002326

    DrmA DNA binding, DrmA ATP hydrolysis, and DrmAB heterodimer formation are required for DISARM activation and phage targeting.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-022-30673-1 DNA binding, and DrmAB heterodimer formation are essential for phage targeting by DISARM Bravo et al. established the structural and functional importance of the DrmA-DrmB core complex.
  • DISARM host DNA methylation negatively regulates DISARM DrmAB activation RO:0002212

    DISARM host-DNA methylation limits DrmAB activation by self DNA.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-022-30673-1 methylated DNA limits the ATPase activity of DrmAB which is essential for anti-phage defense Bravo et al. support methylation-sensitive DrmAB activation as a self/non-self filter.
  • DISARM 5-prime-overhang DNA sensing activates DISARM DrmAB activation RO:0002213

    Unmethylated 5-prime-overhang DNA dislodges the DrmA trigger loop to activate DrmAB.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-022-30673-1 binding to DNA substrates containing a 5′ overhang dislodges the TL, initiating a long-range structural rearrangement for DrmAB activation Bravo et al. structurally support 5-prime-overhang-triggered DrmAB activation.
  • DISARM DrmAB activation mitigates phage DNA circularization METPO:2007407

    Activated DISARM blocks early phage DNA circularization and replication after phage entry.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41564-017-0051-0 phi3T was not able to circularize its genome or form detectible lysogens in DISARM-containing cells Ofir et al. showed that DISARM blocks an early intracellular step after phage adsorption.
  • DISARM DrmAB activation confers DISARM system METPO:2007700

    DrmAB activation realizes DISARM-mediated anti-phage defense.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-022-30673-1 provide the mechanism of targeting a wide range of phage by DISARM Bravo et al. linked DNA-interacting DrmA residues to DISARM anti-phage protection in vivo.
  • DISARM system is a phage defense system rdfs:subClassOf

    DISARM possession is a methyltransferase-associated phage-defense-system trait.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41564-017-0051-0 Our results establish DISARM as a new defence system Ofir et al. establish DISARM in the phage defense system family.
