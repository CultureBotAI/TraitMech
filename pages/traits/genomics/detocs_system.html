traitmech:000302 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism possesses a Detocs locus represented in DefenseFinder by DtcA and DtcB profiles plus optional DtcC-family profiles, with an ATP nucleosidase output that can degrade ATP and dATP upon phage infection and halt phage propagation.
