DRT system

traitmech:000279 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism possesses a defense-associated reverse transcriptase locus whose RT-domain component or components can confer bacteriophage defense and are modeled by DefenseFinder as DRT subtypes.

DRT loci use reverse transcriptases for antiphage defense

Conservative system-level sketch linking a DRT locus to reverse-transcriptase active-site dependence, subtype-specific bacteriophage resistance, and the DRT system trait.

NONMECHANISTIC · The graph captures DRT systems at the family level while leaving the direct RT products, phage triggers, subtype-specific partner proteins or non-coding RNAs, additional subtype mechanisms, and exact phage targets unresolved.

  • defense-associated RT activity contributes to subtype-specific phage resistance RO:0002326

    Conserved reverse-transcriptase active-site residues are required for DRT-mediated defense activity.

    • DOI:10.1126/science.aba0372 In all cases, mutations in the RT active site [(Y/F)xDD to (Y/F)xAA, where x is any amino acid] abolished activity Gao et al. show DRT antiphage activity requires RT active-site residues.
  • DRT locus contributes to subtype-specific phage resistance RO:0002326

    Experimentally reconstructed DRT candidate systems provide protection against dsDNA phages.

    • DOI:10.1126/science.aba0372 six of these candidates (UG1, UG2, UG3, UG8, UG15, and UG16) provided robust protection against dsDNA phages Gao et al. support DRT loci as defensive against bacteriophages in heterologous reconstruction assays.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.aba0372 the UG3 (drt3a) and UG8 (drt3b) RTs are components of the same defense system (DRT type 3), with both RTs required for defense activity Gao et al. support multi-component DRT type 3 as one DRT-system subtype.
  • subtype-specific phage resistance confers DRT system METPO:2007700
