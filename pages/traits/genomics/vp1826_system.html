VP1826 system

traitmech:000488 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism possesses a VP1826-family locus represented by the DefenseFinder VP1826__VP1826 custom HMM profile and experimentally linked to reduced bacteriophage plaquing when the cloned Vibrio parahaemolyticus RIMD 2210633 vp1826 cassette is expressed from a VSV105 plasmid.
