CapRel system

traitmech:000244 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism possesses a fused CapRel toxin-antitoxin locus encoding an N-terminal toxSAS toxin domain and a C-terminal antitoxin sensor domain that together can restrict bacteriophage propagation.

CapRel sensors couple phage trigger binding to translation blockade

Evidence-backed process sketch linking a fused CapRel toxin-antitoxin locus to direct phage-protein sensing, toxSAS activation, tRNA pyrophosphorylation, translation inhibition, and restricted phage propagation.

NONMECHANISTIC · The graph captures CapRelSJ46 major-capsid-protein and Gp54 trigger evidence without claiming one universal CapRel phage trigger, allosteric route, escape route, or phage specificity across all fused CapRel homologs.

Edge evidence

  • CapRel locus contributes to CapRel toxSAS activation RO:0002326

    Fused CapRel loci encode the N-terminal toxin domain and C-terminal antitoxin sensor domain that keep CapRel inhibited until phage trigger binding.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41586-022-05444-z regulates the toxic N-terminal region, serving as both antitoxin and phage infection sensor Zhang et al. support the C-terminal CapRel domain as both antitoxin and phage-infection sensor.
  • phage trigger binding activates CapRel toxSAS activation RO:0002213

    Binding of phage-encoded triggers to the C-terminal CapRel sensor relieves autoinhibition of the toxSAS domain.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41586-022-05444-z major capsid protein binds directly to the C-terminal domain Zhang et al. identify phage major capsid protein as a direct CapRelSJ46 trigger.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41586-024-08039-y directly binds and senses two completely unrelated and structurally different phage proteins using the same sensor domain Zhang et al. show that CapRelSJ46 can directly sense an alternative Gp54 trigger.
  • CapRel toxSAS activation contributes to tRNA pyrophosphorylation RO:0002326

    CapRel trigger binding activates the toxSAS domain that pyrophosphorylates tRNAs.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41586-022-05444-z This open state enables CapRelSJ46 to pyrophosphorylate tRNAs Zhang et al. connect relief of autoinhibition to CapRel toxSAS-mediated tRNA pyrophosphorylation.
  • tRNA pyrophosphorylation contributes to translation blockade RO:0002326

    tRNA pyrophosphorylation blocks cellular translation during CapRel antiviral activation.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41586-022-05444-z pyrophosphorylate tRNAs, which blocks translation Zhang et al. identify translation blockade as the immediate outcome of CapRel-mediated tRNA pyrophosphorylation.
  • translation blockade contributes to restricted phage propagation RO:0002326

    Translation blockade contributes to restriction of the infecting phage.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41586-022-05444-z blocks translation to restrict viral infection Zhang et al. connect translation blockade to restriction of viral infection.
  • restricted phage propagation confers CapRel system METPO:2007700

    CapRel-dependent restriction of phage propagation realizes the CapRel system trait.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41586-022-05444-z fused CapRels can provide anti-phage defence Zhang et al. support fused CapRels as anti-phage defense systems.
  • CapRel system is a phage defense system rdfs:subClassOf

    CapRel system possession is a phage-defense-system trait.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41586-022-05444-z a fused anti-phage toxin-antitoxin system Zhang et al. place CapRelSJ46 in the anti-phage toxin-antitoxin-system family.
