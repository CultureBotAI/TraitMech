CmdTAC system

traitmech:000335 · CLASS · PROPOSED

An abortive infection system in which an organism possesses a cmdTAC toxin-antitoxin-chaperone locus whose CmdC chaperone senses viral capsid proteins and whose CmdA antitoxin degradation liberates the CmdT ADP-ribosyltransferase to modify messenger RNA, arrest translation, and inhibit bacteriophage replication.

Trait evidence (12)

  • https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11618068/
    Here we characterize the widely distributed anti-phage defence system CmdTAC, which provides robust defence against infection by the T-even family of phages

    Vassallo et al. name CmdTAC as a widely distributed anti-phage defense system that protects against T-even phages.

  • https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11618068/
    was discovered in the genome of the wild E. coli isolate ECOR22 through a functional genetic screen for anti-phage defence systems

    CmdC detects infection by sensing viral capsid proteins, ultimately leading to the activation of a toxic ADP-ribosyltransferase effector protein, CmdT

    CmdC senses phage infection through viral capsid proteins and activates the CmdT ADP-ribosyltransferase effector.

  • https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11618068/
    CmdT modifies the N6 position of adenine in GA dinucleotides within single-stranded RNAs, leading to arrest of mRNA translation and inhibition of viral replication

    Vassallo et al. support CmdT modification of single-stranded RNA as the translation-arresting antiphage effector output.
