AbiC system

traitmech:000320 · CLASS · PROPOSED

An abortive infection system in which an organism possesses an abiC-family locus represented by the DefenseFinder AbiC__AbiC profile and exemplified by the Lactococcus lactis subsp. lactis ME2 pTN20 determinant whose abiC structural gene confers Prf abortive resistance to small isometric-headed phage p2, reducing plaquing, plaque size, and burst size while killing most infected Prf-positive cells.

AbiC Prf activity aborts lactococcal phage p2 infection

Conservative system-level sketch linking an abiC-family locus to Prf abortive-infection activity, reduced p2 phage propagation, Prf-positive infected-cell death, and AbiC-system possession without asserting an unresolved molecular target.

NONMECHANISTIC · The graph captures AbiC as a named DefenseFinder single-profile abortive-infection system whose ME2 pTN20 prototype restricts small isometric-headed phage p2. It leaves the direct phage trigger, AbiC molecular function, cell-death route, natural locus breadth, and phage escape routes unresolved.
