Prevots et al. support AbiN as a chromosomal lactococcal abortive infection gene, the FEMS review retains AbiN among confirmed lactococcal Abi-like systems, and DefenseFinder represents AbiN with a mandatory AbiN__AbiN profile. The FEMS table leaves the affected phage lytic-cycle step, phage escape mutants, and mechanism of cell arrest or death unknown, and the FEMS structural discussion predicts a putative ligand-binding sensor fold while explicitly calling for further research on the AbiN mechanism. This first system-level record therefore leaves the direct phage trigger, sensor ligand, exact molecular activity, growth-arrest or cell-death route, and phage escape routes unresolved.
