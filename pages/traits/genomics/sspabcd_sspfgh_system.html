SspABCD-SspFGH system

traitmech:000223 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phosphorothioate defense system in which an organism possesses an SspABCD-SspFGH locus that pairs an SspABCD-family single-stranded DNA phosphorothioation module with an SspFGH restriction module to damage non-phosphorothioated phage DNA and suppress phage DNA replication.

SspABCD-SspFGH systems couple Ssp PT marks to foreign-DNA damage

Evidence-backed process sketch linking an SspABCD-SspFGH locus to single-stranded host DNA phosphorothioation, SspFGH-dependent damage of non-phosphorothioated phage DNA, and reduced phage DNA replication.

NONMECHANISTIC · The graph captures SspABCD-SspFGH restriction at the system level without asserting one universal host taxon, PT frequency, a DNA-damage chemistry, a complete SspFGH subunit cycle, or relationship to SspE and archaeal PT antiviral systems.
