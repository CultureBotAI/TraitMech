PD-Lambda-5 system

traitmech:000385 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism possesses a PD-Lambda-5 locus represented by DefenseFinder as a two-profile model, PD-Lambda-5__PD-Lambda-5_A and PD-Lambda-5__PD-Lambda-5_B, and experimentally linked to T2, T4, T6, LambdaVir, SECphi17, SECphi18, SECphi27, T3, and T7 protection when expressed in E. coli.

PD-Lambda-5 loci restrict T2, T4, T6, LambdaVir, SECphi17, SECphi18, SECphi27, T3, and T7

Conservative system-level sketch linking possession of a PD-Lambda-5 locus to protection against T2, T4, T6, LambdaVir, SECphi17, SECphi18, SECphi27, T3, and T7.

NONMECHANISTIC · The graph captures PD-Lambda-5 as a named DefenseFinder phage-defense system while leaving natural host breadth, the direct phage trigger, the effector mechanism, and exact PD-Lambda-5 profile-to-protein correspondence unresolved.
