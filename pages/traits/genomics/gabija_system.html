Gabija system

traitmech:000215 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A genomics trait describing possession of a Gabija antiphage defense locus whose GajA and GajB proteins assemble into a complex that inhibits bacteriophage replication.

Gabija GajAB complexes deplete nucleotides and cleave DNA

Evidence-backed process sketch linking a Gabija gajA-gajB operon to GajAB complex assembly, nucleotide depletion, DNA cleavage, and inhibition of bacteriophage replication.

NONMECHANISTIC · The graph captures the characterized Gabija GajA/GajB outputs without asserting one universal phage sensor, DNA substrate, GajA:GajB stoichiometry, host death trigger, or anti-Gabija evasion mechanism across all Gabija loci.

Edge evidence

  • Gabija gajA-gajB operon contributes to GajAB complex assembly RO:0002326

    The gajA and gajB genes encode proteins that assemble into the Gabija GajAB antiphage complex.

    • DOI:10.1093/nar/gkad951 GajB interacts with GajA to form a heterooctameric Gabija complex Oh et al. support assembly of B. cereus VD045 GajA and GajB into a stable complex.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41586-023-06855-2 create a 4:4 GajA-GajB assembly (hereafter, GajAB) that is essential for phage resistance in vivo Antine et al. support the GajAB assembly as required for phage resistance.
  • GajAB complex assembly contributes to Gabija nucleotide depletion RO:0002326

    The Gabija complex enables GajB-mediated nucleotide hydrolysis and nucleotide-pool depletion.

    • DOI:10.1016/j.chom.2023.06.014 GajA requires GajB, which senses DNA termini produced by GajA to hydrolyze (d)A/(d)GTP, depleting essential nucleotides Cheng et al. connect the GajA/GajB complex with depletion of adenine and guanine nucleotide pools.
  • GajAB complex assembly contributes to Gabija DNA cleavage RO:0002326

    The Gabija GajAB complex catalyzes GajA-mediated DNA cleavage during antiviral defense.

    • DOI:10.1016/j.chom.2023.06.014 synergy between GajA-mediated DNA cleavage and nucleotide hydrolysis by GajB initiates efficient abortive infection defense Cheng et al. support DNA cleavage as a GajA-dependent component of Gabija defense.
  • Gabija nucleotide depletion mitigates phage replication METPO:2007407

    Gabija nucleotide depletion helps inhibit bacteriophage replication.

    • DOI:10.1016/j.chom.2023.06.014 depleting essential nucleotides. This ATPase activity of Gabija complex is only activated upon DNA binding Cheng et al. connect GajB ATPase activity to depletion of nucleotide pools required for phage replication.
  • Gabija DNA cleavage mitigates phage replication METPO:2007407

    Gabija DNA cleavage helps inhibit bacteriophage replication.

    • DOI:10.1038/s41586-023-06855-2 Gabija proteins assemble into a supramolecular complex of around 500 kDa that degrades phage DNA Antine et al. support phage-DNA degradation as an output of the GajAB complex.
  • Gabija DNA cleavage confers Gabija system METPO:2007700

    GajAB DNA cleavage is part of the antiviral output that realizes the Gabija system defense phenotype.

    • DOI:10.1016/j.chom.2023.06.014 synergy between GajA-mediated DNA cleavage and nucleotide hydrolysis by GajB initiates efficient abortive infection defense Cheng et al. support GajA-mediated DNA cleavage as part of the Gabija defense phenotype.
  • Gabija system is a phage defense system rdfs:subClassOf

    Gabija system possession is a phage-defense-system trait.

    • DOI:10.1093/nar/gkad951 Gabija is one of the most abundant prokaryotic antiviral systems Oh et al. place Gabija among prokaryotic antiviral systems.
