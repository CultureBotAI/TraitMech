Tab system

traitmech:000413 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism or resident prophage possesses a Tab virion-assembly-inhibition locus that expresses the Tab tail-assembly-blocking protein, blocks assembly of invading phage tails, prevents infectious virion formation, and protects the bacterial community from phage spread.
