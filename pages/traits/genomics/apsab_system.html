ApsAB system

traitmech:000328 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A genomics trait describing possession of an ApsAB anti-plasmid defense locus encoding the nuclease/helicase ApsA and Argonaute-like ApsB proteins that can destabilize high- and low-copy-number plasmids.
