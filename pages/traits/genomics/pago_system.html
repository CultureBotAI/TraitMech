prokaryotic Argonaute defense system

traitmech:000419 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A genomics trait describing possession of a prokaryotic Argonaute-centered defense locus that uses a pAgo protein, alone or with cognate accessory proteins, to defend against plasmids, bacteriophages, or other mobile genetic elements.

Prokaryotic Argonaute systems include SPARTA and DdmDE defense loci

Nonmechanistic family sketch linking the pAgo possession trait to experimentally curated SPARTA and DdmDE child systems.

NONMECHANISTIC · The graph is deliberately limited to family hierarchy because long pAgo, SPARTA, DdmDE, SIR2-APAZ, TIR-APAZ, and other APAZ-associated pAgo systems can differ in effector chemistry, guide origin, accessory proteins, and plasmid or phage target breadth.
