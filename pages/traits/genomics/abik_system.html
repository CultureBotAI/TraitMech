AbiK system

traitmech:000229 · CLASS · PROPOSED

An abortive infection system in which an organism possesses an abiK locus encoding a reverse-transcriptase-related polymerase that uses conserved RT motifs for phage resistance and restricts 936/P335 lactococcal phage propagation.

Trait evidence (8)

  • DOI:10.1128/aem.63.4.1274-1283.1997
    The phage resistance mechanism encoded on pSRQ800 is a temperature-sensitive abortive infection system (Abi)

    Emond et al. localized a lactococcal plasmid-encoded abortive-infection mechanism that strongly resists 936 and P335 phages.

  • DOI:10.1128/aem.63.4.1274-1283.1997
    orf1 (renamed abiK) coded for a predicted protein of 599 amino acids (AbiK)

    Emond et al. identified abiK as the open reading frame needed for the pSRQ800 Abi phenotype.

  • DOI:10.1128/aem.63.4.1274-1283.1997
    No phage DNA replication nor phage structural protein production was detected in infected AbiK+ L. lactis cells

    Emond et al. support an AbiK-associated block at or before DNA replication for the ul36 lactococcal phage.

  • DOI:10.1099/00221287-146-2-445
    Only immature forms (concatemeric and circular DNA) of phage p2 DNA were found, indicating that the presence of AbiK prevented phage DNA maturation

    Boucher et al. show that AbiK can block later DNA maturation rather than DNA replication itself, depending on the lactococcal phage tested.

  • DOI:10.1128/JB.187.11.3721-3730.2005
    the antiphage activity depends on the level of expression of the abiK gene and on the presence of a reverse transcriptase (RT) motif in AbiK

    Fortier et al. connect AbiK phage resistance to conserved reverse-transcriptase motifs in the AbiK protein.

  • DOI:10.1128/JB.187.11.3721-3730.2005
    Conservative mutations in key positions resulted in the complete loss of the resistance phenotype

    Fortier et al. mutated AbiK RT motifs and showed that key positions are necessary for AbiK-mediated resistance.

  • DOI:10.1093/nar/gkr397
    AbiK does not exhibit the properties expected for an RT, but polymerizes long DNAs of ‘random’ sequence, analogous to a terminal transferase

    Wang et al. biochemically characterized AbiK as an RT-related polymerase with template-independent DNA polymerization activity.

  • DOI:10.1093/nar/gkr397
    Mutagenesis experiments indicate that the polymerase activity resides in the RT motifs and is essential for phage resistance in vivo

    Wang et al. support AbiK RT-motif polymerase activity as an essential contributor to phage resistance.
