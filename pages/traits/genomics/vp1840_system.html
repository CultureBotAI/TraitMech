VP1840 system

traitmech:000450 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism possesses a VP1840-family locus represented by the DefenseFinder VP1840__VP1840 custom HMM profile and experimentally linked to reduced bacteriophage plaquing when the cloned Vibrio parahaemolyticus RIMD 2210633 vp1840 cassette is expressed from a VSV105 plasmid.
