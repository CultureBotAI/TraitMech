NLR-like bNACHT system

traitmech:000408 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism possesses an NLR-related bacterial NACHT locus represented by DefenseFinder as an NLR_like_bNACHT01 or NLR_like_bNACHT09 single-profile model, encoding a NACHT-module STAND-family protein that can restrict bacteriophage production.

Trait evidence (12)

  • https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10294775/
    Here we show that proteins containing a NACHT module, the central feature of the animal nucleotide-binding domain and leucine-rich repeat-containing gene family (NLRs), are found in bacteria and defend against phages.

    Kibby et al. support NACHT-module proteins as bacterial NLR-related proteins that defend against phages.

  • https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10294775/
    Genome context of bNACHT01, which is located near a CBASS system in Klebsiella pneumoniae MGH 35.

    Kibby et al. identified a NACHT-module gene in Klebsiella pneumoniae MGH 35 before naming it bNACHT01.

  • https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10294775/
    We named this gene bacterial NACHT module-containing protein 1 (bNACHT01), and it did not appear to be in an operon with any other genes.

    Kibby et al. named the selected NACHT-module gene bNACHT01.

  • https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10294775/
    bNACHT01 conferred over a 100-fold increase in protection against phage T4 and over a 1000-fold increase in protection against phages T5 and T6

    bNACHT01 protected against phages T4, T5, and T6 in the E. coli heterologous assay.

  • https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10294775/
    bacteria expressing bNACHT01 restricted phage virion production and continued growing

    Kibby et al. observed restricted phage virion production in bacteria expressing bNACHT01.

  • https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10294775/
    Mutation of R214 to alanine maintained expression of the protein but abrogated phage defense

    An R214A mutation preserved bNACHT01 expression but abrogated defense, supporting a NACHT-dependent output.

  • https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10294775/
    Diverse bacterial NACHT proteins from different clades exhibited robust antiphage activity across a wide range of phages

    Kibby et al. observed antiphage activity across diverse bacterial NACHT proteins.
