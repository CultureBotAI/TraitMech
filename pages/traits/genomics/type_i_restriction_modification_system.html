type I restriction-modification system

traitmech:000494 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A restriction-modification system in which an organism possesses a Type I R-M locus encoding a pentameric enzyme with HsdR-like restriction, HsdM-like methylation, and HsdS-like DNA sequence-recognition subunits, including RM_Type_I loci represented by DefenseFinder.

Trait evidence (7)

  • DOI:10.1093/nar/gkt847
    Type I restriction enzymes (REases) are large pentameric proteins with separate restriction (R), methylation (M) and DNA sequence-recognition (S) subunits.

    Loenen et al. define Type I restriction enzymes as pentameric systems with distinct restriction, methylation, and sequence-recognition subunits.

  • DOI:10.1093/nar/gkt847
    Type I R–M enzymes are pentameric proteins of composition 2R+2M+S.

    Loenen et al. describe the Type I R-M enzyme complex as a 2R+2M+S pentamer with both restriction-endonuclease and methyltransferase activities.
