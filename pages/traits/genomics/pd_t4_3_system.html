traitmech:000388 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism possesses a PD-T4-3 locus represented by DefenseFinder as a single-profile model, PD-T4-3__PD-T4-3, and experimentally linked to T2, T4, and T6 protection when expressed in E. coli.
