SspABCD-SspE system

traitmech:000222 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phosphorothioate defense system in which an organism possesses an SspABCD-SspE locus that pairs an SspABCD single-stranded DNA phosphorothioation module with the SspE restriction enzyme to sense host phosphorothioate marks and nick invading DNA.

SspABCD-SspE systems couple Ssp PT marks to DNA nicking

Evidence-backed process sketch linking an SspABCD-SspE locus to single-stranded host DNA phosphorothioation, SspE PT-site recognition, SspE GTPase-linked nuclease activation, and reduced phage DNA replication.

NONMECHANISTIC · The graph captures SspABCD-SspE restriction at the system level without asserting one universal host taxon, PT frequency, DNA motif outside 5'-CPSCA-3'-characterized systems, full SspE conformational cycle, or relationship to SspFGH and archaeal PT antiviral systems.
