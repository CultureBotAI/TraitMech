VP1817 system

traitmech:000487 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism possesses a VP1817-family locus represented by the DefenseFinder VP1817__VP1817 custom HMM profile and experimentally linked to reduced bacteriophage plaquing when the cloned Vibrio parahaemolyticus RIMD 2210633 vp1817 cassette is expressed from a VSV105 plasmid.
