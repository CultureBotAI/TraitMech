AbiI system

traitmech:000316 · CLASS · PROPOSED

An abortive infection system in which an organism possesses an abiI-family locus represented by the DefenseFinder AbiI__AbiI profile and exemplified by the Lactococcus lactis M138 pND852 locus whose single abiI open reading frame restricts lactococcal phage propagation by an abortive-infection mechanism.

AbiI activity restricts lactococcal phage burst size

Conservative system-level sketch linking an abiI-family locus to AbiI antiphage activity, reduced lactococcal phage burst size, restricted phage propagation, and abortive-infection system possession without asserting the unresolved molecular target.

NONMECHANISTIC · The graph captures AbiI as a named DefenseFinder single-profile abortive-infection system whose pND852 prototype restricts phi 712 and partially restricts phi c2. It leaves the direct phage trigger, AbiI molecular function, cell-death or packaging route, natural locus breadth, and phage escape routes unresolved.
