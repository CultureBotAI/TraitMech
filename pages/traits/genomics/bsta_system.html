BstA system

traitmech:000301 · CLASS · PROPOSED

An abortive infection system in which an organism possesses a BstA-family phage-defense locus whose encoded BstA protein can suppress lytic phage DNA replication and whose cognate anti-BstA aba element can self-immunize the encoding prophage from BstA activity.

BstA loci suppress lytic phage DNA replication through abortive infection

Conservative system-level sketch linking possession of a BstA phage-defense locus to suppressed lytic phage DNA replication, cognate aba-mediated self-immunity, and the BstA system trait without asserting the unresolved direct phage trigger, BstA molecular target, or mechanism of aba-mediated self-immunity.

NONMECHANISTIC · The graph captures BstA as a named abortive-infection system with a DefenseFinder BstA profile while leaving the direct trigger of BstA localization, the direct DNA replication target, the physical form of the BstA-aba interaction, and the molecular basis for cognate aba specificity unresolved.
