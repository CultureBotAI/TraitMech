MspJI system

traitmech:000510 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A type IV modification-dependent restriction system in which an organism possesses an MspJI-family Mrr-like locus encoding a restriction endonuclease that recognizes methylcytosine- or hydroxymethylcytosine-modified DNA and cleaves both strands at a fixed distance from the modified cytosine.
