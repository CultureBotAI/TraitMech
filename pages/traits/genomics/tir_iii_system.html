TIR-III system

traitmech:000481 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism possesses a TIR-III locus, represented in the pinned DefenseFinder HMM inventory by TIR-III__TIR-III_A and TIR-III__TIR-III_B custom profiles, that can inhibit bacteriophage plaquing.

Trait evidence (5)

  • DOI:10.1038/s41467-024-51738-3
    Twelve systems have defense activities against a variable number of phages, and 9 of them are new defense systems and named TIR-I to IX

    Wang et al. report that TIR-III belongs to nine newly named TIR-domain anti-phage defense systems with activity against variable phage subsets.

  • DOI:10.1038/s41467-024-51738-3
    The defense activity of the TIR systems against each phage was determined based on their ability to inhibit plaque formation compared to the pSEC1 empty vector control

    Wang et al. assayed cloned Escherichia coli TIR systems by efficiency-of-plating reduction against a phage panel.

  • DOI:10.1038/s41467-024-51738-3
    TIR-I, TIR-III, and TIR-IV systems appear in diverse domains contexts, whereas the TIR-VII and TIR-VIII systems were found in a single form

    Wang et al. found that TIR-I, TIR-III, and TIR-IV systems occur in diverse domain contexts, while TIR-VII and TIR-VIII systems were recovered in a single form.
