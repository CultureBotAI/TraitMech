SDIC4 system

traitmech:000421 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism possesses an SDIC4 locus encoding an SDIC4A component and a VasI-like SDIC4B component that can reduce adsorption of invading bacteriophages.

Trait evidence (6)

  • DOI:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.115055
    SDIC4 comprises SDIC4A, a protein of unknown function, and SDIC4B, harboring the VasI-like domain

    Cummins et al. describe the SDIC4 locus as encoding an SDIC4A component of unknown function and a VasI-like SDIC4B component.

  • DOI:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.115055
    SDIC4-mediated anti-phage activity is more modest (R10- to 200-fold) but still significant

    Cummins et al. report significant SDIC4-mediated anti-phage activity when SDIC4 was expressed in E. coli.

  • DOI:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.115055
    In the presence of full-length SDIC4 or SDIC4B, we observed a decrease in the adsorption rate

    Cummins et al. connect full-length SDIC4 and SDIC4B with a decreased bacteriophage adsorption rate.
