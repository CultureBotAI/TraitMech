GmrSD system

traitmech:000506 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A type IV modification-dependent restriction system in which an organism possesses a GmrSD locus encoding either separate GmrS and GmrD proteins or a fused double-domain GmrSD-family protein, that targets glucosylated hydroxymethylcytosine-containing DNA.

GmrSD restricts glucosylated HMC DNA
