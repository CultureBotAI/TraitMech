Rst_Hydrolase-3Tm system

traitmech:000402 · CLASS · PROPOSED

A phage defense system in which an organism possesses a two-protein Rst_Hydrolase-3Tm locus represented by DefenseFinder as a two-profile Rst_Hydrolase-Tm model requiring Rst_Hydrolase-Tm__Hydrolase and Rst_Hydrolase-Tm__Hydrolase-Tm and experimentally linked to T7 protection when expressed in Escherichia coli.
