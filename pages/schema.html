TraitMech - Microbial Trait Schema

LinkML schema for modeling microbial ecophysiological traits and phenotypes. Each TraitRecord is a single trait (e.g. "pH optimum", "flagellar arrangement", "growth temperature minimum") with provenance back to its METPO class and optional cross-references to PATO / GO / NCIT / ENVO / CHEBI. Records are seeded from METPO (https://w3id.org/metpo) and curated incrementally, mirroring the dismech / CultureMech / MIM workflow: one YAML per trait, optional evidence-bearing audit trail, validation via `just validate-all`.

Generated by just gen-pages from the LinkML schema src/traitmech/schema/mech_shared.yaml, src/traitmech/schema/traitmech.yaml. Slots are listed as LinkML resolves them for each class, with imports and slot usage applied. Descriptions are shown as written in the schema, without links: the identifiers in them are often format examples. Schema licence: BSD-3-Clause.

TraitRecord

A single microbial trait (phenotype, capability, or quality) with provenance back to its METPO source class. One YAML per record.

Distinct from the causal graphs (which model *how* the trait works, taxon-agnostically): these answer *who* canonically exhibits it. The replaced record remains resolvable with mapping_status: DEPRECATED; this field carries the structured forward link from the new record to the old. The reverse direction (deprecated → replacement) is the OBO/IAO `IAO:0100001` (term replaced by); we use dcterms:replaces because the field lives on the *new* record and points to the *old*.

Enumerations (15)

DiscussionKindEnum

Kind of unresolved / in-progress item captured by a Discussion. Knowledge gaps are represented as a discussion kind so they reuse the shared pointer, evidence, and lifecycle machinery, while optional proposed experiments capture how a gap could be resolved.

Defined in the shared module src/traitmech/schema/mech_shared.yaml.

OPEN_QUESTION
An unresolved scientific question posed by curators or experts.
KNOWLEDGE_GAP
A missing causal, evidentiary, model-system, or measurement assertion whose resolution would materially improve the record.
CONTROVERSY
A live disagreement or competing interpretation between published positions.
CURATION_TODO
A curation task captured inline (e.g. "ingredient needs CHEBI refinement").
EMERGING_HYPOTHESIS
A recently reported hypothesis under active discussion in the community.
INTERPRETATION
A discussion about how to interpret existing evidence or model an edge.
HUMAN_MODEL_MISMATCH
A gap where evidence exists in one system but its fidelity to the target context is uncertain (e.g. an in-vitro/model result whose transfer to the in-situ or host-associated setting is unverified).

A bioenergetic or molecular state of the cell (e.g. proton motive force, redox state of the quinone pool) that mediates a trait mechanism. Distinct from a process: the state is the gradient / steady-value, not its establishment.
QUALITY
A quality or measurable attribute of a cellular component (e.g. membrane fluidity, GC content, motility speed) — a PATO-style axis attached to a microbial entity that modulates the trait mechanism.
CAPACITY
A metabolic or functional capacity — an electron-donating pool, ATP charge, or other reservoir-of-X notion that is neither a single chemical species nor a process. Examples include reducing power (NADH/NADPH pool) and energy charge.
